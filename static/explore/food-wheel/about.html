Scope and evidence

This dated research visualisation represents the ADHD-focused BRAIN framework snapshot prepared on 6 November 2026: 90 foods, 68 substance identities and 121 substance–system relationships. It includes qualified contextual and candidate relationships, including local research mappings which are not necessarily present on the currently published canonical PM pages.

Diagram inclusion is not dietary admission. Composition, precursor conversion, biological participation and intervention outcomes are distinct claims. Connections and target counts do not establish dose adequacy, tissue delivery, clinical benefit or ADHD efficacy.

Twenty-one proposed mapping records remain proposals. Eighteen proposed-only food–substance pairs are hidden until enabled. Other proposals qualify existing pairs. Proposed mappings are not canonical scientific admissions.

Food positions reflect the circular centre of their distinct documented target positions, with equal target weighting and even spacing. Ribbon colours follow the target’s BRS/BRS-X relationships; multicolour bands indicate several systems. Food quantities do not determine position or ribbon width.

Direct and derived targets remain distinguished in details. Collective pools coexist with individual constituents, so counts measure ontology connectivity rather than independent biochemical functions. Quantities are ranked only within comparable analytical groups; presence-only and derived sources are not concentration rankings.

Primary references, preparation conditions and source limitations are retained in the wheel and accompanying source tables. No new clinical or dietary admission is made by publication of this visualisation.
