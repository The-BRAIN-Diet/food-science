Method, evidence selection and limitations

The ranked layer reuses current PM shared-selector observations and powers. Threshold controls filter that selection; they do not rerank or alter admissions. Supporting sources come only from existing eligible PM projections when a substance has no ranked source at the chosen threshold. Up to two foods per substance are shown by default: measured records first, then preparation-qualified, qualitative and related routes; canonical IDs break ties. This prioritises documentation completeness, not clinical strength. Expansion retains eligible alternatives.

Individual essential amino acids take precedence over the EAA aggregate in additive target counts; all relationships remain traceable. Related pathways are not direct downstream supply. Whole egg and egg yolk use separate composition records. Choose the form eaten when comparing contributions.

Typical servings and safe frequencies are not established in this shared dataset. Entered portions are calculation scenarios, not recommendations. Dry-weight, formation-assay, qualitative and related observations are not converted into serving doses.
