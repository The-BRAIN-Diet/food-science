## Claim decisions

The Overview retains GAD-dependent conversion of glutamate to GABA, PLP as the
required cofactor, and the adjudicated distinction between reserve capacity and
basal output. It separates synthesis from GABA concentration, degradation,
transport, receptor signalling, glutamate clearance and excitotoxic buffering.
Mason is used to bound concentration from synthesis rate. Biological relevance
is retained without converting vitamin B6 dependency or ADHD pool findings into
a treatment claim.

## Sufficiency and stopping rationale

Mason; Edden) directly adjudicate the reaction, cofactor interpretation and
measurement boundary. No external retrieval was performed. Search stopped
because every retained Overview proposition resolves to existing assessed
evidence and no defining synthesis step remained unassessed.

## Unresolved claims

synthesis. GABA concentration findings in ADHD do not establish altered
synthesis capacity or nutritional treatment benefit.
