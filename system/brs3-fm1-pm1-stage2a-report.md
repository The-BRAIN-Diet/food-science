## Question the ontology asks

What sets NF-κB transcriptional tone: the kinase step that releases NF-κB, or a dietary pattern?

## Corpus, then search

Attached papers were read before any new search. Mercurio et al. 1997 (Science 278:860–866) purified the IκB kinase complex from cytokine-stimulated HeLa cells, identified IKK-1 and IKK-2, and showed that IKK-2 mutants change RelA nuclear translocation and an NF-κB reporter. Adams 2001 is a general protein-kinase review. It is not an IKK assay.

Targeted search for an IKK-specific magnesium requirement found kinase-assay buffers that contain magnesium chloride, and a structural model in which magnesium ions bridge ATP phosphates. Those are not a dietary magnesium trial and not an ATP–Mg crystal of IKK. UniProt IKBKB, already used in the prior 2B, records the ATP reaction without a magnesium cofactor annotation. Magnesium stays a biochemical participant, not a new finding of dietary necessity.

Zelicha 2022, Li and Zhang 2026, Cannataro 2024, Tongjaroenbuangam 2011, Camuesco 2006, Batey 2024, Marsland 2017, and Brown 2025 do not measure IKK activity or NF-κB transcription rate. They stay off the finding.

## Findings

- PM1-F1. IKK phosphorylation is required for NF-κB activation. Primary: Mercurio 1997. Adams 2001 is connected support for ATP chemistry and is excluded as an IKK or diet result.
