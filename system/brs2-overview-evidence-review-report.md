# BRS2 Opening-Copy Evidence Review

## Scope and contracts

This pass reviewed all 13 BRS2 mechanism pages on 30 September 2026:

- seven Primary Mechanisms;
- three Functional Mechanisms;
- one Specific Mechanism; and
- two Key Constraints.

The seven PMs were assessed under the current **Evidence-supported Overview
(Stage 2A)** contract. FM, SM and KC pages retained their own opening-copy
contracts: Mission and Overview with three orientation or cross-BRS bullets for
FMs and the SM, and one concise Ambition paragraph for KCs. The PM format was
not imposed on other entity types.

Existing copy, summaries and attached references were treated as propositions,
not authority. Existing repository evidence was sufficient for the bounded
claims retained here, so no external retrieval was required.

## Primary Mechanism Overview decisions

### BRS2-FM1-PM1 — Folate/B12-dependent homocysteine remethylation

The Overview now states the methionine-synthase reaction, its position upstream
of SAMe, and the human plasma-homocysteine endpoint supported by Aragão et al.
(2024) and the Homocysteine Lowering Trialists' Collaboration (1998).
“Rate-limiting ceiling” and broad oxidative/inflammatory outcome language were
removed. Plasma homocysteine lowering is explicitly separated from
tissue-specific flux, methylation output and clinical benefit.

### BRS2-FM1-PM2 — Betaine/BHMT remethylation

The Overview retains BHMT-mediated betaine donation, direct or
choline-derived betaine provision, and supplemental-betaine lowering of plasma
homocysteine. “Backup resilience” and high-folate-demand claims were removed.
The page now separates BHMT flux, downstream SAMe synthesis and treatment
outcomes.

### BRS2-FM1-PM3 — SAMe synthesis

This PM already had authored Findings. The Overview was reconciled to PM3-F1,
PM3-IC1, PM3-F2 and PM3-F3: MAT forms SAMe from methionine and ATP; magnesium
and potassium participate in catalysis; SAMe concentration and SAM:SAH describe
pool balance rather than isolated MAT flux. Dietary provision, supplementation
and downstream methyltransferase action remain separate claims.

### BRS2-FM1-PM4 — Methionine-cycle flux

The Overview now presents cycle-wide production, use, recycling and branch
allocation. SAM, SAH and homocysteine are bounded as integrated pool markers
unless flux tracing is available. Dietary “balance,” methylation-potential
improvement and treatment conclusions were not admitted into the Overview.

### BRS2-FM2-PM5 — Transsulfuration pathway

The Overview retains irreversible homocysteine-to-cysteine routing, the CBS/CGL
steps and their pyridoxal-phosphate dependence. It no longer states that redox
burden directly activates the pathway. Observed vitamin-B6 associations are
separated from measured transsulfuration flux and from supplementation
outcomes.

### BRS2-FM2-PM6 — Glutathione synthesis

The Overview now defines the two ATP-dependent synthesis reactions and the
glutamate, cysteine and glycine substrate set. Human precursor-response evidence
is bounded to the studied older-adult population. Glutathione utilisation,
selenium-dependent peroxide removal, recycling, neuronal protection and
clinical outcomes are outside the synthesis claim.

### BRS2-FM3-PM7 — Phosphatidylcholine formation

The Overview now centres on the three SAMe-dependent PEMT methyl transfers and
the resulting phosphatidylcholine pool. It separates hepatic formation from
blood–brain transport and neuronal DHA incorporation. Piglet accretion and
human cerebrospinal-fluid measurements are retained only as downstream
delivery context, not as direct measurements of PEMT flux.

## PM contract result

Every BRS2 PM now has:

- a concise, cited explanatory paragraph;
- exactly three non-duplicative bullets covering mechanism boundary,
  evidence/measurement boundary, and biological relevance/treatment
  limitation;
- claim-local links to the intended numbered bibliography records; and
- a frontmatter summary aligned with the paragraph without citation markup.

Only BRS2-FM1-PM3 currently has authored Scientific Findings. PM1, PM2, PM4,
PM5, PM6 and PM7 therefore remain **Overview-only bounded assessments**, not
completed Stage 2A mechanism reviews. No Finding objects were inferred or
invented from inherited prose.

## Functional Mechanism opening decisions

### BRS2(FM1) — Methylation Cycle Efficiency

The opening now describes integrated one-carbon recycling and methyl-donor
handling without claiming broad epigenetic, mitochondrial or clinical effects.
Its three bullets retain orientation to the host BRS and supported downstream
domains.

### BRS2(FM2) — Transsulfuration & Redox Coupling

The opening retains homocysteine disposal, cysteine availability and
glutathione-substrate coupling. It no longer implies that pathway activity
alone determines resilience or recovery.

### BRS2(FM3) — Methylation–Membrane Coupling

The opening retains SAMe-dependent phosphatidylcholine formation and
membrane-lipid context. Brain omega-3 delivery and receptor-function claims
were narrowed to contextual, downstream relationships rather than direct FM
outcomes.

All three FM summaries were aligned with their opening paragraphs. Their
three-bullet FM orientation format was preserved; PM boundary bullets and
mandatory §1 citations were not imposed.

## Specific Mechanism opening decision

### BRS2(SM-SNP1) — Genetic Modifiers of One-Carbon Metabolism

The opening now frames inherited variants as context-sensitive modifiers of
established pathways, not new pathways or deterministic outcome predictors.
Opening-level ADHD susceptibility, nutritional responsiveness and prescribing
implications were removed. The legacy SM Mission and Overview format and three
orientation bullets were preserved.

## Key Constraint Ambition decisions

### BRS2(KC1) — Methyl Donor Pool

The Ambition remains one concise paragraph focused on adequate one-carbon donor
availability for remethylation and methyl-group transfer. Outcome and
supplementation claims remain in evidence-qualified downstream sections rather
than the opening.

### BRS2(KC2) — Methionine & Transsulfuration Substrate Pool

The Ambition remains one concise paragraph focused on adequate, balanced sulfur
amino-acid substrates for methionine-cycle flux, transsulfuration and
glutathione synthesis. It does not imply methionine restriction, higher intake
or longevity treatment.

## Coverage sufficiency and stopping rationale

The attached corpus covered the reaction identities, substrates, principal
branch boundaries and measurement ceilings needed for opening-copy
orientation. Claims that would have required broader outcome or regulated-flux
evidence were removed or narrowed instead of being preserved through adjacent
literature. Retrieval stopped at the attached corpus because no retained
opening claim remained dependent on an unsupported central proposition.

## Unresolved and deferred

- Six BRS2 PMs still require full Stage 2A Scientific Finding authoring.
- Surrounding Mission, Primary Biological Effects, Mechanistic Basis, lever and
  Phenome copy was not globally rewritten by this opening-only pass.
- Direct human tissue flux remains unresolved for several remethylation,
  transsulfuration, glutathione-synthesis and PEMT propositions.
- Non-PM pages remain governed by their entity schemas and should not be
  described as having completed the PM Stage 2A Overview contract.
