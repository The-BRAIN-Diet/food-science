## Propositions under test

Mechanistic Basis:

1. Serotonin modulates mood stability, inhibition and behavioural regulation.
2. Serotonin formation depends on tryptophan hydroxylation (iron, BH4, TPH2)
   and PLP-dependent decarboxylation.
3. Meal-level precursor transport is upstream presentation, not serotonin
   signalling.
4. After synthesis, signalling includes receptors, reuptake and metabolism.

Phenome relationships:

5. Serotonergic signalling supports Emotional Regulation.
6. Weaker or adjacent mappings (stress, sleep, reward, worry, pleasure, social
   engagement) must not be restated as dietary or saffron-treatment findings.

## Supported claims

- Serotonin is a multi-receptor neuromodulator relevant to emotional
  regulation, inhibition and related control.
- Tryptophan is the pathway starting material. Tryptophan hydroxylase is the
  first and rate-limiting step and requires iron and BH4. TPH2 is the
  neuronal isoform. PLP supports decarboxylation of 5-hydroxytryptophan to
  serotonin.
- LAT1 / plasma tryptophan-to-LNAA ratios change precursor presentation.
  That is not serotonin signalling.
- Release, receptor families, SERT-mediated reuptake and enzymatic metabolism
  are part of the signalling sequence.

## Constrained claims

- Cofactor or substrate necessity is not intake-responsive human brain
  serotonin, a mood benefit, or an ADHD treatment effect.
- ADHD-related serotonin findings are heterogeneous and are not a uniform
  deficit.
- BRS2 methylation / SAM is a constrained connection: pineal conversion of
  serotonin toward melatonin can use SAM, but the assessed sources do not
  establish that ordinary one-carbon variation controls serotonergic
  signalling.
- Saffron supplementation and systemic-inflammation connectivity are not
  serotonin-process measurements.

## Rejected claims

- Changing tryptophan, iron or vitamin B6 has been shown to raise brain
  serotonin or treat mood, sleep or ADHD.
- Plasma LNAA ratios are measurements of serotonin signalling.
- Saffron is a Dietary Requirement of serotonergic signalling.

## Unresolved claims

- Direct human dietary modification of serotonin synthesis, storage, release
  or receptor signalling.
- Ordinary-diet dependencies of MAO, SERT and vesicular handling
  (`CC-BRS1-FM1-PM5-01` / `FW023` — Deferred — evidence assessment pending).

## Questions and stopping rationale

Questions:

1. Is tryptophan hydroxylase the first step from tryptophan to 5-HTP, and
   what does it require?
2. Is TPH2 the neuronal enzyme?
3. After synthesis, what stages constitute signalling?
4. Do attached ADHD, nutrition, saffron or inflammation sources measure
   serotonin signalling after ordinary diet?

Stopped after Fitzpatrick, Walther, Fanet, Berger and the inherited reviews
adjudicated those questions. Did not expand into every receptor-subtype or
SSRI-pharmacology paper. Schema validation is not scientific completion.
