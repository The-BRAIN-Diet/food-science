## Stage 2A evidence disposition

Canonical PM12 contains eleven Findings and eighteen references. Every Evidence Considered record includes study, population, result, magnitude, summary, limitations and retrieval scope. Finding confidence remains `not-yet-scored`; no Phenome ratings were invented.

| Finding | Evidence and measured endpoints | Disposition / boundary |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | [Xu 2009](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08389): longitudinal motor-learning spine imaging | Formation and selective stabilisation in mice. Spine observations are not independently verified synapse counts. |
| F2 | [Bosch 2014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2014.03.021), [Harward 2016](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature19766): spine volume, actin/scaffold trafficking and local signalling | Supports structural machinery. Slice stimulation and protein markers are not dietary effects or cognition. |
| F3 | [Yang 2014](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1249098), [Li 2017](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.4479), [de Vivo 2017](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aah5982) | Distinguish post-learning formation, REM-dependent pruning/retention and EM contact resizing; contexts differ. |
| F4 | [Chen 2012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2012.02.030): inhibitory gephyrin imaging, spine dynamics and original ultrastructural validation | Includes inhibitory-contact remodelling. A marker-defined contact is not a universal functional-synapse census or circuit E/I assay. |
| F5 | [Schafer 2012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2012.03.026): microglial engulfment, activity and complement manipulation | Developmental visual-circuit pruning. Not an adult diet or anti-inflammatory intervention. |
| F6 | [Sakamoto 2007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brainres.2007.08.089), [Ulus 2006](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10571-006-9004-5), [Cansev 2005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brainres.2005.07.054), [Wurtman 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brainres.2006.03.019) | Separates nucleotide/headgroup provision, phospholipid/protein abundance and spine density. Supply-to-structural-assembly interpretation is not measured mediation. |
| F7 | [Stranahan 2007](https://doi.org/10.1002/hipo.20348): Golgi/DiI morphology after voluntary running | Adult rat regional structural response, not neurogenesis alone or a human exercise prescription. |
| F8 | [Gupta 2016](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1002563): presynaptic active-zone structure, release and fly memory | Spermidine altered ageing-related organisation, but did not rescue reduced synapse numbers. [Schwarz 2022](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2022.13875) is connected human null memory evidence, not a structural test. |
| F9 | [Liston/Gan 2011](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1110444108): glucocorticoid-dependent spine turnover | Context-dependent hormone biology; not a named stress-management practice. |
| F10 | [Carbone 2020, online 2019](https://doi.org/10.1093/cercor/bhz083): developmental cortical markers, cultured contacts/network activity and visual responses | Preserve each endpoint and developmental context; marker abundance is not EM verification of every contact. |
| F11 | [Chakraborty 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2025.105783): longitudinal regional spine maintenance with dietary scheduling, activity and memory | Conditional mouse obesogenic context; regionally opposite responses are not a universal synapse-increasing effect. |

**Sakamoto precision:** Figure 2 reports DHA-alone spine density +19%, DHA plus UMP +36% versus control, and no significant UMP-alone spine-density effect. All diets contained choline. The dose experiment's 16–20 sampled neurons are not 16–20 independent animals. The null result limits standalone structural intervention claims; it does not invalidate the independently supported nucleotide precursor role. Choline's background presence is not an isolated choline intervention.

**Context separation:** healthy learning/activity, developmental pruning and cortical maturation, ageing active-zone changes and injury protection are distinct. No generic injury-repair admission or human structural-growth claim is made.

No one end-to-end human assay or deficiency experiment was required to accept provision.
