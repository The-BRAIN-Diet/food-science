# BRS2-FM1-PM1 Folate/B12-Dependent Homocysteine Remethylation — Stage 2A Report

## Scope

Stage 2A adjudicated the methionine synthase reaction before any Dietary Requirement was admitted. Existing Overview, Mechanistic Basis, phenome rationales and the eight attached references were treated as propositions. Mission was the scope to assess, not evidence that the mechanism achieves it.

The Overview was already bounded. It was changed only to replace a citation that did not support the reaction and to stop calling folic acid a generic “remethylation cofactor.” The claim ceiling is unchanged: reaction identity, a circulating homocysteine marker, and no treatment claim. The public paragraph is 69 words, with exactly three bullets. Mission was left as written.

## Propositions and dispositions

| Proposition | Disposition |
|---|---|
| Methionine synthase converts 5-methyltetrahydrofolate + homocysteine to methionine + tetrahydrofolate, using methylcobalamin | Supported. Froese et al. (2019), including the stated reaction homocysteine + CH3-THF → methionine + THF. PM1-F1. |
| Riboflavin/B2 is a cofactor of methionine synthase | Rejected. The catalytic cofactor is methylcobalamin. PM1-F2. |
| Riboflavin/FAD belongs to upstream MTHFR, and also to MTRR repair of inactive cobalamin | Supported as neighbouring enzymology, not as a synthase cofactor. García-Minguillán et al. (2014). PM1-F2. |
| Plasma homocysteine lowering is tissue remethylation flux | Rejected as an equivalence. PM1-IC1. |
| Separation from BHMT | Supported. BHMT is a separate, mainly liver and kidney remethylation route using betaine. PM1-F1 and the PM2 connection. |
| Separation from MAT/SAMe | Supported. MAT is the next reaction, methionine + ATP → SAMe. PM1-F1 and the PM3 connection. |
| Separation from transsulfuration | Supported. Cystathionine beta-synthase is the first transsulfuration step. Vitamin B6 did not add homocysteine lowering beyond folic acid and B12 in Collaboration (1998). |
| Separation from PEMT | Supported as a boundary. Phospholipid methylation consumes SAMe later. It is not the synthase reaction. Stated in PM1-F1 limitations and the cross-BRS membrane connection. |
| ADHD or cognitive-decline studies establish this reaction | Not established. Held as phenome-relationship findings PM1-F3 and PM1-F4. |

## Retrieval

Questions:

1. What is the methionine synthase reaction, and is methylcobalamin the cofactor?
2. Is riboflavin/FAD a cofactor of methionine synthase, or only of MTHFR and of MTRR?
3. Do the attached trials measure tissue remethylation flux or blood homocysteine?
4. Are BHMT, MAT, transsulfuration and PEMT the same reaction?

Sources used:

- Attached corpus, tested first (table above).
- Froese, Fowler and Baumgartner (2019), already in `BRAIN-diet.bib` as `froese_vitamin_2019`. Full review text read for the reaction, cofactor, folate-cycle route to 5-methyltetrahydrofolate, cobalamin processing, BHMT, MAT and transsulfuration. `bounded-external-search` because it was not previously attached to this PM.
- García-Minguillán et al. (2014), PMID 25322900. New sidecar entry `garcia_minguillan_riboflavin_2014`. Read for the explicit split: FAD on MTHFR, FMN and FAD on MTRR, methylcobalamin on methionine synthase, plus the blood-homocysteine interaction (8.2%; interaction p = 0.048).

Stopping rationale: the reaction, the B12 cofactor, the B2-versus-MTHFR/MTRR split, the blood-marker ceiling, and the four neighbouring-reaction boundaries are each supported or rejected. Further ADHD, fish-oil, or cognitive-outcome papers would not identify the enzyme. A bacterial zinc/potassium literature lead was not promoted: the human-pathway review does not assign those ions, and an unpublished domain deposit was not used. That catalytic-ion question is unresolved and is not a reason to reopen the reaction.

## Connections

Published links now carry a direction:

- BRS1-FM2-PM6: parallel methyl route. This reaction uses 5-methyltetrahydrofolate rather than betaine, so it does not supply the choline used for acetylcholine. The older animal brain-choline claim was not restated here; those studies remain on the acetylcholine PM.
- BRS1-FM3-PM7: downstream use. Methionine can later become SAMe for phospholipid methylation. Not a synthase substrate step.
- BRS2-FM1-PM2: parallel route to methionine via betaine.
- BRS2-FM1-PM3: downstream use of methionine by MAT.
- BRS2-FM1-PM4: shared cycle context, not identity with the synthase step.

No upstream PM supplies 5-methyltetrahydrofolate as a separate mechanism record. Methylenetetrahydrofolate reductase is an enzyme step inside folate provision, not a sibling PM dependency to display beside a dietary label.
