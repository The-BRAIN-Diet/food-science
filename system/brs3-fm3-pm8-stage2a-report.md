## Search

Human intake to circulating mediators: Barden et al. 2014 (J Lipid Res 55:2401–2407). Twenty-one healthy adults took 2.4 g a day of n-3 fatty acids. By day 5, RvE1, 18-HEPE, 17-HDHA, and 14-HDHA had risen. Other measured specialised pro-resolving mediators did not. Aspirin from day 5 added no further rise.

Mas 2012 reported concentrations after supplementation and was not needed once Barden provided the before-and-after comparison. A marine-oil study that already contained preformed mediators was not used, because that exposure is not EPA and DHA as substrates the body converts.

## Findings

- PM8-F1. Resolvins and protectins are made from EPA and DHA. Serhan and Petasis 2011.
- PM8-F2. Five days of 2.4 g a day raised some of those mediators in healthy adults. Barden 2014.

The previous statement that increasing intake had not been shown is withdrawn for the mediators Barden measured. It remains true that not every mediator rose and that disease resolution was not measured.
