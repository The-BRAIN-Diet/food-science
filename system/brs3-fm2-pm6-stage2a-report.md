## Distinct job

Vitamin E on PM5 breaks peroxyl-radical chains in the membrane. Vitamin E on this page is the radical that vitamin C reduces. Those are different jobs, so the same nutrient can be named on both pages.

## Corpus, then search

Packer et al. 1997 is a chapter that places vitamin E in a network regenerated by other antioxidants, especially thiols. It is not the direct observation of the vitamin C reaction. Vertuani 2004 is a general antioxidant review. Klein 2011 is a cancer endpoint, not recycling flux.

Targeted retrieval: Packer, Slater and Willson 1979 (Nature 278:737–738) observed ascorbate reducing the vitamin E radical by pulse radiolysis. Niki 2014 states that the synergistic interaction of vitamin E and vitamin C extends vitamin E capacity. On this page that sentence is the recycling claim. The chain-breaking claim stays on PM5. Niki is primary in one finding here, so no within-page dependency note is required.
