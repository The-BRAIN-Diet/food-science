## BRS5-FM1-PM2 - LPS / Endotoxin Containment

(Limiting Host Exposure to Inflammatory Microbial Products)

## 1. Mission & Overview

### Mission

Limit host exposure to bacterial endotoxin and related inflammatory microbial products.

### Overview

Lipopolysaccharide is part of the outer surface of many bacteria. A higher concentration in blood means more of this endotoxin has reached the circulation. In mice, four weeks of a diet providing 72% of energy as fat raised plasma lipopolysaccharide about 2.7-fold, a change the authors called metabolic endotoxaemia. [[1]](#pm-ref-1) In adults with metabolic syndrome, plasma lipopolysaccharide rose in the hours after a saturated-fat meal challenge and did not rise after the other fat challenges in that trial. [[2]](#pm-ref-2)

- **Benefits:** Containing this exposure helps limit how much biologically active endotoxin reaches host tissues. The studies measured plasma lipopolysaccharide after defined fat exposures. [[1]](#pm-ref-1) [[2]](#pm-ref-2)
- **Biological Relevance:** Epithelial tight-junction structure is a different job; see PM1.

## 2. Primary Biological Effects

Plasma lipopolysaccharide can rise after defined high-fat exposures. Lipopolysaccharide-binding protein was unchanged in the metabolic-syndrome fat trial. [[1]](#pm-ref-1) [[2]](#pm-ref-2)

## 4. Mechanistic Basis

### Summary

Plasma lipopolysaccharide is the exposure followed here. A very high-fat diet raised it in mice. [[1]](#pm-ref-1) In adults with metabolic syndrome, it rose after a saturated-fat meal challenge and did not rise after the other fat challenges in that trial. [[2]](#pm-ref-2)

<div class="brs-fm-hub-item" data-brs-fm-hub>
<div class="brs-fm-hub-shell">
<button type="button" class="brs-fm-hub-summary" aria-expanded="false">
<span class="brs-fm-hub-chevron" aria-hidden="true"></span>
<strong>Circulating lipopolysaccharide and metabolic endotoxaemia</strong>
</button>
<div class="brs-fm-hub-panel" hidden>

In mice, four weeks of a diet providing 72% of energy as fat raised plasma lipopolysaccharide about 2.7-fold. Cani et al. (2007) called that sustained rise metabolic endotoxaemia. [[1]](#pm-ref-1) A 40% fat diet produced a smaller rise, about 1.4-fold, and was not the main arm. A subcutaneous infusion of lipopolysaccharide reproduced several metabolic features of the high-fat diet. That infusion is not a meal. [[1]](#pm-ref-1)

In 75 adults with metabolic syndrome, 12 weeks on one of four fat patterns was followed by a matching fat challenge. Plasma lipopolysaccharide rose in the four hours after the saturated-fat challenge only. Lipopolysaccharide-binding protein did not change at fasting or after the meals. López-Moreno et al. (2017) infer greater intestinal absorption. That route was not measured directly. [[2]](#pm-ref-2)

A separate contrast in healthy lean adults used 10 people per meal. A 910 kcal high-fat, high-carbohydrate meal raised plasma lipopolysaccharide. An American Heart Association meal rich in fibre and fruit did not. Ghanim et al. (2009) also reported Toll-like receptor and NF-κB binding changes after the high-fat meal. Those signalling readouts are a different job; see BRS3-FM1-PM2. The meal was mixed, so fibre is not isolated. [[3]](#pm-ref-3)

A review discusses both circulating lipopolysaccharide and changes in the gut lining. Mohammad and Thiemermann (2021) do not themselves measure the mouse or meal results above. The lining is a different job; see PM1. [[4]](#pm-ref-4)

### 5.2 Cross-BRS Mechanism Relationships

Primary Mechanisms in other Biological Regulatory Systems that directly interact with, constrain or support this mechanism.

- [BRS3-FM1-PM2 - Gut-Derived Inflammatory Signalling](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm1/brs3-fm1-pm2-gut-derived-inflammatory-signalling) — circulating endotoxin can feed inflammatory signalling. This mechanism follows the exposure. The cytokine response is a different job.
- [BRS6-FM1-PM3 - Insulin Sensitivity & Glucose Disposal](/docs/biological-targets/brs6/fm1/brs6-fm1-pm3-insulin-sensitivity-and-glucose-disposal) — in mice, the high-fat rise in plasma lipopolysaccharide was accompanied by features of insulin resistance. [[1]](#pm-ref-1) That is not a human insulin-sensitivity result.

### 5.3 Local BRS Mechanism Relationships

Related Primary Mechanisms within the same Biological Regulatory System that collectively support the integrated biological function.

- [BRS5-FM1-PM1 - Gut Barrier / Tight Junction Integrity](/docs/biological-targets/brs5/fm1/brs5-fm1-pm1-gut-barrier-tight-junction-integrity) — the physical state of the gut lining is one possible route into the blood. This mechanism measures circulating lipopolysaccharide; see PM1.
- [BRS5-FM1-PM3 - Microbial Barrier–Immune Interface Support](/docs/biological-targets/brs5/fm1/brs5-fm1-pm3-keystone-taxa-support) — mucus and defined microbial activities are a neighbouring job. They are not a measured lipopolysaccharide result here; see PM3.
