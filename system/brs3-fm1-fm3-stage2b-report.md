## PM3 dietary requirement and optimisation pass

**5 October 2026.** Section 3.1 was run again for glucoraphanin-derived sulforaphane. Section 3.2 was then run because that candidate had induction evidence and had failed as a requirement.

| Candidate | Section | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sulforaphane / glucoraphanin | 3.1.1 Direct or Derived | Not admitted | Nrf2 is the cell’s own gene switch. Houghton shows broccoli-derived sulforaphane can induce it. Induction is not a substrate or cofactor the switch requires, and the review sets no intake. |
| Vitamin C | 3.1.3 Key constraint | Unchanged | Stays antioxidant-substrate context. It is not the inducer. |
| Oral broccoli-derived sulforaphane | 3.2 Conditional supplementation | Admitted as PM3-SOP-1 | Houghton reviews oral doses as capable of modifying Nrf2-linked cytoprotective genes, and discusses sulforaphane-yielding supplements. That is the exposure actually reviewed. Limitation: no dose, no crucifer serving pattern, and chemoprevention gene changes are not a cognitive outcome. Cited from PM3-F1. |

The 5 October supplement classification and the “repeated exposure” sentence were superseded the same day after the papers were read. The record of that source check is FW043–FW045. Sulforaphane remains not a Direct or Derived requirement.

**Costa et al. 2026 considered the same day.** Narrative review of natural and synthetic Nrf2 activators in inflammatory diseases ([PMC12974331](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12974331/)). It does not reopen section 3.1: sulforaphane is still an inducer, not a substrate or cofactor the switch requires. Synthetic activators in that review are medicines and stay off this page.

| Candidate from Costa 2026 | Section | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sulforaphane as a requirement | 3.1.1 | Not admitted | The review calls it an activator. The switch does not require it. |
| Oral broccoli-derived sulforaphane | 3.2, existing PM3-SOP-1 | Kept, limitation widened | Houghton remains the induction evidence. Costa adds the bound: oral sulforaphane did not significantly change Nrf2 expression in people with COPD, and the same cruciferous intake can produce about a fivefold difference in circulating sulforaphane. |
| Myrosinase-active crucifer preparation | 3.2 Food preparation | Not admitted | Cooking is said to inactivate myrosinase, and conversion depends on the microbiota. That is a delivery constraint, not a tested preparation with an Nrf2 result. |
| Broccoli sprouts for gastritis | 3.2 | Not admitted | A disease outcome in infected mice and humans. It is not this gene-switch practice. |
| Curcumin, resveratrol, and the synthetic drugs | 3.1 and 3.2 | Not admitted | Other activators in an inflammatory-disease review. They are not this exposure, and the drugs are outside dietary levers. |

| Candidate | Section | Decision | Why |
