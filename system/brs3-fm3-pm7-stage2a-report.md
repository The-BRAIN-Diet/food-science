# BRS3-FM3-PM7 Stage 2A — 6 October 2026

## Question

Are IL-6 and TNF-α the messengers of this step, and did any attached trial change them?

## Corpus

Slavich and Irwin 2014 is an integrative review. It places IL-6 and TNF-α in stress-related inflammatory signalling toward depression. It does not measure NF-κB transcription, and it is not a diet trial.

Ferguson et al. 2014 (the FFAME trial) randomised 60 healthy adults to placebo or EPA plus DHA at 900 mg or 3600 mg a day for eight weeks, then gave intravenous endotoxin (0.6 ng/kg). High-dose EPA plus DHA reduced the temperature rise (p = 0.03). IL-6, TNF-α, and the other individual inflammatory markers were not statistically different from placebo. A pattern of lower responses was described. A pattern is not a significant change in these messengers.

The endotoxin challenge is a way to read the messengers. It is not evidence that omega-3 fats set the network. EPA and DHA stay off this page as requirements. They are substrates on PM8.

## Finding
