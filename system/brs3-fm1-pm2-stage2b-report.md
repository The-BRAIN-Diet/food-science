# BRS3-FM1-PM2 Stage 2B — 6 October 2026

## Withdrawn

PM2-DIT-1 Fermentable fibre was a Direct dietary-provision atom. The papers cited for it (Cavaliere, Grüter, Li) do not show fibre supplying the substrate, and Koh 2016, which does, does not show that fibre limits endotoxin spillover. Precursor fermentation is not this governed capacity. The atom is withdrawn.

PM2-DIT-2 Butyrate was a biochemical requirement. Exogenous butyrate in other models is not a requirement of lipopolysaccharide translocation. Withdrawn. Butyrate is no longer listed as a cofactor.

## Admitted

No Direct, Derived, or biochemical requirement. No System Optimisation Practice. No lifestyle lever. Dominance is Not established. `timing_specific` is No.

## Key constraint

BRS3(KC1), the glutathione precursor sufficiency iKC, stays unresolved. Public copy remains “No mapping established.” The historical Antioxidant Substrate Sufficiency title is not published.

## Searches that did not clear admission

Prebiotic fibre and lipopolysaccharide-binding protein in young adults: no overall reduction. Fibre meta-analysis of serum lipopolysaccharide: heterogeneity too high, and lipopolysaccharide-binding protein did not fall. Not admitted.
