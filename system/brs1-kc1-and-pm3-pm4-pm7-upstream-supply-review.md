## Governing distinction

The dietary-input-traceability contract, canonical iKC section (line 1887 at review), defines **Supported upstream supply** as “a source-supported pathway maintains or replenishes a resource used by this PM”. Deficiency evidence and a demonstrated limiting effect are not mandatory. **Conditional constraint** requires evidence of resource availability constraining the PM in a specified context. A supported mechanistic chain may suffice; there is no universal single end-to-end human assay requirement.

Type D explicitly applies the capacity threshold only to Conditional constraint. §3.1.1 retains independently admitted Direct/Derived requirements, §3.1.2 biochemical participants, and §3.1.3 KC pools and individually adjudicated constituents. Membership does not imply applicability or additional-intake benefit. Identical provision disclosures must be consolidated while preserving evidence and provenance.

Legacy whole-pool/arm iKC references are not constituent identities. The KC evidence-review contract's older one-pool-until-declared convention is reconciled here through explicit constituent-v2 registration under the current KC schema. Historical PM records keep their original meaning; none is silently converted to a constituent admission.

## Evidence retrieved and measurement boundaries

1. **Lotta et al. (1995)** — [primary PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7703232/), DOI 10.1021/bi00013a008. Recombinant human soluble/membrane-bound COMT kinetics establish SAM use while methylating dopamine and noradrenaline. This is not a dietary or regional neural clearance study. Abstract inspected; full methods/results not obtained. No dietary effect estimate inferred.
2. **Klatt et al. (2019)** — [primary full article](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007114519002009), Results and Figures 5–7 inspected. Figure 5: no choline main effect on total plasma LPC-DHA. Figure 6C/D: higher d3-LPC and d3-LPC-DHA enrichment with higher choline intake. Figure 7: comparative d3/d9 contributions. d3 identifies the methyl-derived PEMT route; d9 identifies the intact choline/CDP-choline route. Enrichment is not net carrier production, absolute hepatic flux or brain uptake. No claim that higher choline increased total LPC-DHA is justified by this trial.
3. **Nguyen et al. (2014)** — [primary Nature preview](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature13241), abstract and figure descriptions inspected. Figure 3 cell transport, Figure 4 labelled LPC brain uptake in mice. This establishes transport-substrate biology, not donor inadequacy. Subscription full methods/results were not obtained in this pass.
4. Reused **BRS2 PM3-F4** and its explicitly connected PM1/PM2 Findings supply previously reviewed reaction context. Evans 2002 and Bailey 2021 are primary BHMT/MAT evidence. Froese 2019, Obeid 2013 and **Salvi and Gadda 2013 are reviews**; do not relabel them as new primary brain-supply measurements.
5. Targeted primary search identified rat precursor/competition studies including Wurtman et al. 1974 (PMID 4276197). The PubMed page did not provide accessible full results during this pass. That lead was not promoted to verified human or replete dietary-constraint evidence. Native KC membership retains its existing Fernstrom 2013 review appraisal.

## What changed in the interpretation

The older PM propositions tested **inadequacy constraining governed capacity**. An unresolved answer to that stronger question is not a blanket rejection of Supported upstream supply. Both decisions must remain separately traceable. No rule was changed to accommodate an admission.

Native KC1 tyrosine does not justify reprinting PM3's substrate job. COMT methyl-resource supply is different from tyrosine synthesis only because the evidence identifies a different reaction job. PM7 carrier provision is likewise distinct from DHA's structural membrane role, while its limiting effect remains unresolved.
