# BRS input → substance identity audit

8 October 2026. Read-only inspection of canonical content; no scientific admissions, registry edits, food relationships or substance pages created.

## Scope and method

Inspected 64 active canonical PM pages across BRS1–6 and BRS-X, the canonical substance registry and actual substance page frontmatter, plus the existing shared disclosure resolver. Preview/draft routes and retired PMs excluded. Canonical dietary sections 3.1.1/3.1.2/3.1.3 are the lookup identity even when displayed as 1.1.1/1.1.2/1.1.3. A promoted System Optimisation or Lifestyle group is not a substance requirement just because its visible number is 1.1.

The structured inventory contains 188 disclosure occurrences and 93 exact labels after excluding clear practices/protocols. These are labels, not a count of unique chemicals or proposed pages. Pool labels and preparations are retained for review; not automatically converted into substances. Renderer-exposed records are not a fresh scientific admission. Legacy body-only candidates are separately listed below, since missing structured records do not prove exclusion or admission.

Identity verification uses registry key + registry path + page frontmatter ID. Display-name/slug similarity is only a candidate locator. No web research was required to establish what records exist locally; chemical identity and composition research remains a creation step.
