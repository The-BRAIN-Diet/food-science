# BRS3-FM3-PM8 Stage 2B — 6 October 2026

## §3.1.1

PM8-DIT-1 EPA and PM8-DIT-2 DHA stay Direct substrates at dietary-provision. Evidence is now PM8-F1 and PM8-F2 (Serhan and Petasis 2011; Barden 2014). Simopoulos is not a requirement citation.

Limitation, on both atoms: five days at 2.4 g a day in healthy adults raised some mediators, not every mediator, and not a disease-resolution outcome.

## §3.1.2

EPA and DHA are not repeated. They had the same input and the same substrate role as §3.1.1.

## Dominance

The principal route is the dietary requirement (`3.1`), because the supplementation changed some of the mediators this mechanism is about. Diet-Dominant is kept for that reason, not because of an oily-fish slogan. `dose_sensitivity` now records the tested amount. `timing_specific` is No. No lifestyle lever. No System Optimisation Practice: the exposure is the substrate intake, already in §3.1.1, not a separate preparation protocol.

## Not admitted

Cytokine modulation from omega-3 trials is not a second requirement. Aspirin did not add a mediator rise and is not a lever. Preformed specialised pro-resolving mediators in enriched oils were not admitted as substrates.
