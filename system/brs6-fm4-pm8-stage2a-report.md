## Scope and proposition inventory

The mission remains “Limit metabolic-inflammatory signalling from adipose stress so whole-body resource allocation is not overloaded.” It proposes a biological objective rather than a measured intervention outcome. The assessment tests adipose immune/oxidative stress, modulation of adipose load and tissue pathways, dietary component attribution, and the limits of systemic biomarkers. Whole-body allocation and brain outcomes remain unresolved downstream propositions, with an explicit Open Issue; no Phenome association is invented.

| Proposition | Adjudication and source |
| --- | --- |
| Expanded adipose tissue has immune-cell/inflammatory biology | Triangulated human association and experimental cell/mouse evidence; PM8-F7, Weisberg 2003. Human association alone is not universal causal mediation. |
| Acute nutrient excess must cause cytokine inflammation before metabolic dysfunction | Challenged by the acute oxidative-stress study; PM8-F7, Boden 2015. Extreme acute overfeeding is a limited context. |
| Moderate weight loss improves insulin action by first reducing measured adipose inflammation | Not supported for the measured markers and early time point; PM8-F1. Retain Magkos's later pathway changes and small progressive-loss subgroup. |
| A defined green-MED package reduces visceral adipose load | Supported within DIRECT-PLUS; PM8-F3. Visceral volume is a relevant load endpoint, not an assay of tissue inflammatory mediation. |
| The package identifies independently effective foods | Unresolved; PM8-F4/F5 and Open Issues. Both Mediterranean groups received walnuts; component intake associations are not randomized effects. |
| Marine omega-3 formulations can change adipose inflammatory endpoints | Mixed intervention evidence; PM8-F6 includes positive, limited and null tissue results. Delivery does not guarantee target response. |
| More magnesium reduces human adipose stress | Unresolved in this assessment; PM8-F8 includes a primary counter-context, without recommending deficiency or denying general magnesium biology. |
| Movement, sleep and defined timing practices influence adipose endpoints | Context-specific support; PM8-F9/F10/F11. Allocation, exposure severity and selected tissue analyses constrain the claims. |
| These interventions improve brain function through adipose signalling | Not established in this assessment; no direct functional outcome or adipose mediation verified. Open Issue retained. |

## Findings, scope reconciliation and stopping decision

The canonical page now has eleven Findings, each with a precise proposition, the actual study population/exposure, measured result, uncertainty and source recovery limits. PM8-F1 and PM8-F2 identities are preserved. The Overview and Mechanistic Basis now distinguish immune recruitment, oxidative stress, fat volume and circulating markers. They do not equate healthy adipose function with simply suppressing all immune activity.

Mission is retained as the framework objective. Its whole-body allocation and brain-health extension remains an explicit evidence gap, not a demonstrated benefit. No new Phenome mapping, KC identity or numeric score is inferred. The inherited “Diet-Supported” label is supported within the newly stated contexts, while display prominence is separately justified in Stage 2B.

Coverage is sufficient for a **bounded local reassessment with explicit unresolved questions**: the six inherited references have dispositions; the omitted foundational adipose biology and user-nominated dietary package have been assessed; material food-component, tissue, protocol and lifestyle gaps received targeted follow-up including null/counter-context sources. Each remaining question states what prevents a stronger claim and the needed experiment. This is not a complete intervention catalogue, complete replication review or resolution of every downstream mission term. Schema checks certify structural implementation only.
