## §4.1.1 candidates

### Polyphenols (class)

- **Relationship tested:** Direct state-regulation of NF-κB transcriptional tone by a polyphenol class or polyphenol-rich pattern.
- **Evidence:** Zelicha et al. 2022 (DIRECT PLUS; human RCT; green-MED vs MED vs guidelines + physical activity); Tongjaroenbuangam et al. 2011 (quercetin; dexamethasone-treated mice); Camuesco et al. 2006 (quercitrin + olive oil + fish oil; DSS colitis rats).
- **Exposure:** Zelicha — whole-pattern diet (walnuts, green tea, Mankai, less red/processed meat), not an isolated polyphenol. Tongjaroenbuangam — isolated quercetin in animals. Camuesco 2006 — combined flavonoid + n-3 diet in animals.
- **Endpoint:** Zelicha primary/reported outcome is **VAT**, not NF-κB activity or IκB phosphorylation. Tongjaroenbuangam is neuroprotection under glucocorticoid stress. Camuesco 2006 reports colonic inflammatory mediators; NF-κB pathway readouts in this line of work sit mainly in related papers (e.g. Camuesco 2004 / Comalada 2005), not as an attributable ordinary-diet polyphenol class effect on this PM.
- **Direct/Derived:** not admitted.
- **Five atoms:** none.
- **Claim ceiling:** n/a.
- **Limitation / reject reason:** Generic or pattern-level inflammatory/adiposity benefit is not regulation of **this** PM’s governed state. Combination/pattern evidence does not attribute a polyphenol class. Isolated/pharmacological flavonoid exposure is not ordinary food polyphenol exposure.

### EPA/DHA

- **Relationship tested:** Direct state-regulation of NF-κB transcriptional tone by dietary EPA and/or DHA (not SPM-substrate capacity; that is PM8).
- **Evidence considered:** Camuesco et al. 2006 (combined fish-oil diet ± quercitrin, DSS rats). Li and Zhang (2026) FASEB *J.* meta-analysis of 41 RCTs of EPA+DHA **supplementation** in sport (doi:10.1096/fj.202504783R). Cannataro et al. (2024) *Life* 14:1315, cited by Li and Zhang as the source of the NF-κB/SPM mechanistic sentence.
- **Exposure:** Li and Zhang — mixed EPA+DHA **supplements**, strongest effects at ≥2 g/day for ≥6 weeks in recreational athletes. Cannataro — narrative review of omega-3 ONSs and SPM biochemistry in sport. Neither tests ordinary-food EPA/DHA as an isolated NF-κB lever on this PM.
- **Endpoint:** Li and Zhang pooled **IL-6, TNF-α, creatine kinase, and DOMS**. Those are not NF-κB activity, IκB phosphorylation, or NF-κB-target transcription of this PM. The paper’s NF-κB clause is **mechanistic background**, not a meta-analysed outcome. Cannataro discusses resolvin/maresin downregulation of NF-κB as **SPM pharmacology**, citing other reviews.
- **Direct/Derived:** not admitted on PM1. Not inherited from PM8. A Derived “EPA → SPM → NF-κB” chain was not built: the Direct SPM→NF-κB edge is not established as a PM1 Dietary Requirement, and SPM substrate capacity already lives on PM8.
- **Five atoms:** none.
- **Claim ceiling:** n/a.
- **Limitation / reject reason:** Generic post-exercise cytokine/recovery effects ≠ regulation of **this** PM’s governed state. Review-of-review NF-κB language is not dietary EPA/DHA → NF-κB evidence. Combination Camuesco design still blocks constituent attribution. Isolated/high-dose supplement exposure is not ordinary food exposure.

### Fibre-rich whole-food patterns

- **Relationship tested:** Direct state-regulation of NF-κB transcriptional tone by fibre or a fibre-rich pattern.
- **Evidence:** Zelicha 2022 (whole green-MED pattern; VAT); Batey et al. 2024 (LPS–neurotransmission review context).
- **Exposure:** Zelicha pattern is not a fibre-subtype intervention. Batey is not a fibre feeding study.
- **Endpoint:** VAT / LPS-neurotransmission context, not NF-κB transcription.
- **Direct/Derived:** not admitted (not PM2 fermentable-fibre inheritance).
- **Five atoms:** none.
- **Claim ceiling:** n/a.
- **Limitation / reject reason:** No evidence-supported fibre → NF-κB-state edge at the claimed granularity.

In vitro EPA at high micromolar concentration (e.g. C2C12 IκB/NF-κB) and disease-specific or mixed-oil PBMC gene-expression studies were **not** used to close the SOP claim: they do not establish the tested supplement intervention as an evidence-qualified influence on this PM’s governed state, and they must not be silently generalised to food EPA/DHA.

**Not REJECTED:** the literature is biologically suggestive; it does not contradict the proposed route. The missing edges are supplementation → SPM/resolution **response** and supplementation → **this** PM’s NF-κB state.
