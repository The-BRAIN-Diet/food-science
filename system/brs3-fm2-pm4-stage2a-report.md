## Corpus, then targeted retrieval

Attached sources included Mocchegiani and Malavolta (2019), Verlaet et al. (2019), Zhai et al. (2015), Packer et al. (1997), Uribarri et al. (2010), and the ADHD marker papers (Bulut, Kurhan, Miniksar, Dvořáková). Mocchegiani is a zinc and selenium review. It does not establish copper or manganese, and it does not measure generation versus clearance. The ADHD papers measure oxidative markers. They do not measure enzyme flux.

Targeted retrieval stopped at three sources.

- Weydert and Cullen (2010), Nature Protocols 5:51–66, DOI 10.1038/nprot.2009.197. Superoxide dismutase converts superoxide to hydrogen peroxide. Catalase and glutathione peroxidase convert hydrogen peroxide to water. Copper-zinc superoxide dismutase, manganese superoxide dismutase, and glutathione peroxidase as a selenoprotein that requires glutathione are named.
- Xia et al. (2010), American Journal of Clinical Nutrition 92:525–531, DOI 10.3945/ajcn.2010.29642. Adults whose diet supplied about 14 micrograms of selenium a day. Plasma glutathione peroxidase activity levelled off at a total intake of about 35 micrograms a day. Selenoprotein P needed about 49 micrograms a day. Higher doses did not raise glutathione peroxidase further.
- Uauy et al. (1985), Journal of Nutrition 115:1650–1655, DOI 10.1093/jn/115.12.1650. Infants recovering from malnutrition with marginal copper. Copper supplementation raised red-cell superoxide dismutase with plasma copper. Copper added in the assay did not.

Stopping rationale: those three sources name the clearance enzymes, place the metals, and supply the two human deficiency-to-enzyme results used in Stage 2B. A further mineral review would not change the participants or the claim ceiling. Zinc and manganese dietary-deficiency trials that change superoxide dismutase were not found in this search, so those minerals were not given an intake finding. Catalase’s haem iron was recorded and not pursued as a dietary requirement, because no human iron-intake result for catalase was retrieved.
