# BRS2-FM3-PM7 Phosphatidylcholine Formation — Stage 2A Report

## Scope

Stage 2A assessed BRS2-FM3-PM7 against the Profile A contract and the bounded Scientific Finding contract. The governed reaction is hepatic phosphatidylethanolamine N-methyltransferase (PEMT): three S-adenosylmethionine-dependent methylations of phosphatidylethanolamine to phosphatidylcholine. The CDP-choline (Kennedy) pathway is a parallel route. Blood–brain DHA transport and neuronal DHA incorporation remain BRS1-FM3-PM7. Piglet accretion and cerebrospinal-fluid DHA are downstream context, not PEMT flux.

Attached keys were reviewed first: `vance_phospholipid_2014`, `arellanes_brain_2020`, `liu_higher_2014`, `oulhaj_omega-3_2016`, `millichap_diet_2012`, `derbyshire_role_2023`.

## Propositions and dispositions

| Proposition | Disposition |
|---|---|
| PEMT converts phosphatidylethanolamine to phosphatidylcholine by three SAMe-dependent methyl transfers | Supported (PM7-F1). Shields et al. (2003) topography and SAMe-binding papers; Vance review of the titled PEMT article. |
| Substrates are phosphatidylethanolamine and SAMe | Supported (PM7-F1). No metal cofactor is named. |
| Magnesium is a PEMT cofactor | Rejected. The page listed the name only. Vance does not establish it. Retrieved reaction papers specify phosphatidylethanolamine and SAMe. A title search pairing PEMT with magnesium as cofactor or requirement did not produce a mammalian cofactor paper. Yeast divalent-cation data were not used as mammalian proof. |
| S-adenosylhomocysteine inhibits or regulates PEMT | Supported at the level the sources actually state (PM7-F2). Vance: product AdoHcy can regulate activity. Ridgway and Vance (1988): S-adenosylhomocysteine is released after methyl transfer, and product-inhibition patterns fit an ordered mechanism. |
| Elevated homocysteine itself inhibits PEMT | Not supported. The cited sources do not show a homocysteine concentration inhibiting the enzyme. Homocysteine and S-adenosylhomocysteine were not treated as the same measurement. |
| CDP-choline / Kennedy pathway performs this methylation | Rejected as part of this reaction. It is a parallel route. Pemt-knockout mice fail on a choline-deficient diet because both routes are then missing (Vance). |
| Choline is a PEMT substrate | Not supported. Choline feeds the parallel route. |
| PEMT preferentially contributes polyunsaturated species to lipid transport | Not established from the retrieved Vance abstract. Removed from the Overview. Not promoted to a Finding. |
| Piglet cortex DHA accretion and human cerebrospinal-fluid DHA measure PEMT rate | Rejected as a PEMT-rate claim. Retained as an interpretive constraint (PM7-IC1). Liu: about 1.9-fold higher gray-matter accretion from phosphatidylcholine-DHA than triglyceride-DHA; liver labelling was greatest. Arellanes: cerebrospinal-fluid DHA rose about 28 percent versus placebo; both arms received B vitamins; cognition did not differ. |
| Oulhaj B-vitamin by omega-3 cognitive interaction measures PEMT rate | Rejected as a rate claim (PM7-F3). Downstream inference for Cognitive Clarity only. |
| Millichap and Derbyshire measure PEMT or attention via this reaction | Rejected as a PEMT measurement (PM7-F4). Downstream inference for Focus / Attention Stability only. |
| Methyl-donor or methionine-pool inadequacy constrains PEMT capacity | Not established. Recorded for Stage 2B Type D. Substrate necessity and upstream position are not that constraint. |

- Europe PMC abstract of the paper named by the Vance title (PMID 22877991, DOI 10.1016/j.bbalip.2012.07.017). Substrates AdoMet and phosphatidylethanolamine; product AdoHcy can regulate activity; choline-deficient Pemt-knockout phenotype.
- Europe PMC abstracts of Liu et al. (2014) and Arellanes et al. (2020), already attached, read against the PEMT-rate proposition.
- UniProt Q9UBM1 catalytic activities: phosphatidylethanolamine, methylated intermediates and S-adenosyl-L-methionine in; S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine out. No cofactor line for magnesium.

The stored key `vance_phospholipid_2014` carries the correct title and the wrong PMID (22884926, a leishmaniasis paper) and DOI (`10.1016/j.bbalip.2012.07.018`). Adjudication used the paper the title names. The main bibliography was not edited.

## Stopping rationale

Coverage is sufficient for the reaction boundary, the two substrates, rejection of magnesium as a PEMT cofactor, product-level S-adenosylhomocysteine regulation, the parallel choline route, and the measurement boundary for brain DHA and cognition. Further methyltransferase reviews would repeat the same reaction. Quantified S-adenosylhomocysteine inhibition constants were not taken from secondary databases because the Ridgway abstract does not state them. Preferential polyunsaturated-species transport was not asserted. Schema validation is not scientific completion.
