## Stage 2A evidence and scope

Seven Findings replace the four broad published highlights. The original four references remain in the draft bibliography and are bounded to their actual evidentiary function.

| Finding | Measured links | Inferred links / boundary |
|---|---|---|
| PM2-F1 — Intestinal alkaline phosphatase activity is substrate-dependent | Goldberg 2008 and Chen 2010: IAP defence/phosphate-release/inflammatory-response experiments. Komazin 2019: chemically defined LPS/lipid-A substrate access. Igunnu 2011: purified calf IAP zinc/magnesium model-substrate catalysis. | IAP is not demonstrated to dephosphorylate every native enteric LPS. Model enzyme metal dependence does not demonstrate that increasing oral zinc/magnesium improves human LPS clearance. |
| PM2-F2 — Chylomicrons provide an intestinal LPS transport route | Ghoshal 2009: cell and mouse lipid exposures, transport inhibition and labelled LPS uptake. | Intestinal transcellular/lipid-associated transport is distinct from paracellular permeability. Human ordinary-meal transfer magnitude is not established by the mouse model. |
| PM2-F3 — Meal composition and structure alter acute endotoxin readouts | Lyte 2016: 20 healthy adults, different meal lipid mixtures, serum endotoxin. Vors 2017: 8 normal-weight and 8 obese men, equal milk-fat amounts in different physical structures, chylomicron/plasma LPS kinetics. | Comparator, obesity context and timing matter. Lower integrated LPS does not imply every early LPS measurement fell; neither trial establishes health benefit or a universal fatty-acid requirement. |
| PM2-F4 — Specific fermentable exposures change containment readouts in defined models | Cani 2009: oligofructose in obese mice, LPS/permeability and GLP-2 antagonism. Zhang 2020: RS2 in aged female mice, serum/faecal LPS, mucin and metabolites. Human GOS, pectin and butyrate studies retained as limiting evidence. | GLP-2-sensitive mediation is supported in the Cani model. Accompanying butyrate in RS2 does not isolate causal mediation. No whole-class fibre claim or routine human dose follows. |
| PM2-F5 — A tested polyphenol mixture changed a meal-associated LPS response | Ghanim 2011: 10 healthy adults, resveratrol plus muscadine-grape extract before a high-fat/high-carbohydrate meal, plasma LPS/LBP and cellular readouts. | Combination attribution; not evidence for isolated resveratrol, every polyphenol, berries, cocoa or tea. Routine supplementation and clinical benefit remain unestablished. |
| PM2-F6 — Emulsifier studies do not establish one uniform containment effect | Chassaing 2022: CMC exposure, microbiome/metabolome, encroachment in two participants. Wellens 2026 (online 2025): several specified emulsifiers, permeability/SCFA measures; no between-arm LBP/inflammatory effect. | Microbiota change/encroachment is not automatically demonstrated systemic LPS containment failure. Additive emulsifiers are not interchangeable with food-fat emulsification. |
| PM2-F7 — LPS inactivation helps terminate persistent microbial signalling | Lu 2008: AOAH-deficient and wild-type mice, LPS deacylation, tolerance and enzyme restoration. | Lower cytokine responsiveness may be persistent tolerance rather than successful containment. This is inactivation biology, not an admitted dietary lever. |

The PM owns endotoxin passage/transport and inactivation relevant to host exposure. General epithelial maintenance is contextual/upstream; SCFA production stays with its own PM; general downstream inflammatory regulation is connected rather than relabelled containment. Findings are not FM roll-ups and their outcome confidence remains **not yet scored**.

Human GOS/pectin/butyrate findings constrain extrapolation. The precise remaining gap is a source-supported resource/mediator/compensation chain at the pool level and its PM-specific context. - Existing KC1 KIT1/KIT2/KIT3 legacy constituent meanings were inspected, not silently migrated into individually registered iKCs. Pool membership does not confer PM applicability.
- **BRS5(KC2): current combined-pool admission not established.** Its canonical page does not establish a coherent shared pool. A single extract-combination trial does not admit a plant-diversity checklist or every member. This decision does not exclude all polyphenol exposures.
- No admitted KC relationship, iKC matrix projection or FM roll-up is created. §3.1.3 displays **No mapping established.** A new pool disclosure that merely repeats the two fermentable exposure jobs would be duplicate presentation.
- Cross-BRS redox/epithelial-resource hypotheses remain future candidates, not inherited mappings. A separate assessment would have to distinguish barrier/redox support from measured endotoxin transport/inactivation and adjudicate the relevant canonical pool/constituents individually. This pass does not claim an exhaustive rejection of all possible cross-BRS KCs.
