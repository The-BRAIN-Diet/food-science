# BRS3-FM2-PM3 Stage 2A — 6 October 2026

Title and mission stay: Nrf2-Mediated Cellular Defence Regulation. The mission is regulation in response to stress, not a standing instruction to activate the pathway.

## Corpus, then search

Houghton 2016 reviews sulforaphane as an inducer, not as a nutrient the regulation requires. Riedl, Saxon and Diaz-Sanchez 2009: healthy nonsmokers, broccoli sprout homogenate plus daikon myrosinase, three days, nasal phase II enzyme RNA rose with dose; alfalfa did not. Nrf2 protein and brain were not measured. Wise 2016: COPD, 25 or 150 µmol extracted sulforaphane capsules for four weeks, no rise in Nrf2 target genes in alveolar macrophages or bronchial epithelium.

Verlaet 2018 places vitamin C, zinc, and selenium with antioxidant enzymes. Those jobs belong with the mechanisms that use the enzymes, not as Nrf2 requirements. Costa 2026 is preparation variability, not a new trial.

The defining control step was not in the attached corpus as a primary paper. Targeted retrieval: Itoh et al. 1999 (Genes Dev 13:76–86) identified Keap1 as the repressor of Nrf2; electrophiles release that repression. Zhang et al. 2004 (Mol Cell Biol 24:10941–10953) showed Keap1 marks Nrf2 for disposal, and that oxidative stress or sulforaphane slows that disposal. A cysteine-151 mutant resists that effect.

## Findings

- PM3-F1. Keap1 holds Nrf2 until stress lets defence genes be read. Itoh 1999 and Zhang 2004.
- PM3-F2. The homogenate raised airway defence-gene RNA. Riedl 2009. Wise 2016 is the negative capsule result in the same finding.

The old title “Nrf2 activation raises endogenous defence-gene transcription” is replaced so activation is not the goal.
