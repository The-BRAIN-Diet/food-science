# BRS1-FM1-PM3 Dopaminergic Signalling — Stage 2 Evidence Checklist

Status: **Stage 2A evidence integration complete**

Implementation and Dietary Levers handoff:
`system/dopaminergic-signalling-stage2a-report.md`.

This checklist governs the dedicated evidence pass for BRS1-FM1-PM3. Stage 1
establishes scope and architecture only; it does not establish dietary
actionability, Phenome relationships or clinical efficacy.

Evaluate and adjudicate each link separately:

1. **Biological requirement** — identify the evidence establishing the pathway,
   substrate, cofactor, transport, storage, release, receptor, reuptake or
   metabolic proposition.
2. **Habitual dietary inadequacy** — determine whether relevant dietary inputs
   are commonly inadequate in the population under assessment.
3. **Biochemical insufficiency in the target population** — determine whether
   the relevant biochemical state is measurably insufficient in ADHD or another
   explicitly defined target population.
4. **Responsiveness when baseline status is adequate** — determine whether
   additional intake changes the relevant biology when baseline status is
   already adequate.
5. **Modification of dopaminergic biology** — require direct evidence that the
   assessed exposure changes dopamine synthesis, storage, release, receptor
   signalling, reuptake or metabolism.
6. **Functional or clinical effects** — assess attention allocation, reward
   learning, motivation, effort allocation, behavioural activation and
   condition-specific outcomes separately from biochemical change.
7. **Limitations and non-supportive evidence** — retain null findings,
   state-dependent effects, exposure limits, measurement limitations and
   contradictory evidence.
8. **Population and model specificity** — distinguish ADHD evidence from
   general physiology, other conditions, animal models and pharmacology.

Completion of links 1–3 is not evidence for links 4–6.

## Required attribution boundaries

- Do not equate dietary tyrosine provision with increased brain dopamine.
- Do not equate a change in dopaminergic biology with improved ADHD symptoms.
- Do not treat pharmacological manipulation as evidence that diet modifies the
  same target or reproduces the same outcome.
- Keep isolated-nutrient, food, supplement, combination-formulation and
  pharmacological exposure contexts distinct.
- Publish a dietary, cofactor, substance, food, Phenome or cross-BRS
  relationship only through a valid evidence object that supports that exact
  proposition.

## Candidate dependency assessments

- **BRS2:** one-carbon metabolism and SAM-dependent COMT activity.
- **BRS3:** catecholamine oxidation, oxidative burden and antioxidant handling.
- **BRS4:** energetic requirements for synthesis, vesicular handling and
  neuronal signalling.
- **BRS6:** metabolic, stress-related and circadian modulation of dopaminergic
  function.

Each remains a candidate relationship until proposition-specific evidence is
adjudicated. Candidate status must not enter a published mechanical roll-up.
