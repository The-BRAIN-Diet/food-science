# Training: From Primary Mechanisms to BRS Dietary Guidance

**Public module (local-only):** `/docs/training/from-primary-mechanisms-to-brs-dietary-guidance`  
Requires `INCLUDE_INTERNAL_DOCS=true` in `.env.local`. Training content is untracked and excluded from production builds.  
**Audience:** authors, reviewers, practitioners, and technical agents  
**Rule:** teach using live website pages. Do not rewrite PM, FM, KC, Food, Substance, Recipe, or BRS scientific wording on training pages.

## Standing qualifications

1. The rollup is mechanism-led and normally begins with PMs, but not every evidence relationship must originate from a PM. Preserve independently supported Matrix, Pattern, Temporal and System Optimisation evidence at FM or BRS level where assigning it to a PM would overstate mechanistic specificity.

2. Deduplication does not convert repeated mechanistic participation into a dietary requirement. A Substance appearing across several PMs becomes one consolidated resource relationship. It enters BRS dietary guidance only where an evidence-supported dietary intervention or adequacy concern exists.
