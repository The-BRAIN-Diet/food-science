## Governed state and specificity decisions

The PM concerns adipose stress and associated metabolic-inflammatory signalling. Visceral-fat volume is a relevant upstream load endpoint; subcutaneous biopsy markers and blood proteins measure other aspects. The admitted pattern relationship stops at its measured adipose volume and circulating-protein effects. It does not claim that inflammatory tissue mediation, nutritional necessity or brain benefit was established.

For the broad green-diet label: (1) the biological target is adipose load and associated signalling; (2) the food pattern itself was tested; (3) constituent-level narrowing would invent causal attribution. For the broad omega-3 label: the tested formulations and tissue endpoints can be named, while narrowing to one fatty acid or expanding to fish/walnuts would overclaim. For magnesium: the nutrient identity is clear, but a specific accepted PM8 dependency and supply chain is not. The report preserves each of these distinct decisions.

The user explicitly asked to retain low-evidence foods for future testing. They therefore remain visible as named components/candidates with evidence limits. This does not turn observational component associations into independently admitted Dietary Requirements. Pattern quantities are trial exposures, not established individual treatment doses.

## Three dietary layers and KC distinctness

§3.1.1 contains one pattern-level regulatory relationship. B/D biochemical dependency-supply tests are not applicable to that state-regulation claim. The six-atom `dietary_input` value is true because the tested exposure is dietary, not because the pattern is indispensable.

§3.1.2 remains without a newly admitted cofactor/substrate. The original page's inventory was empty; magnesium was nevertheless independently investigated. Generic enzyme participation does not identify a PM8-specific cofactor claim. No intracellular molecule was created as an intake target.

§3.1.3 retains the two existing unresolved KC applicability decisions. KC1's circulating-glucose availability does not automatically establish an adipose-stress supply or constraint. KC2's broad micronutrient/lipid checklist does not define a coherent admitted shared pool. This does not reject all constituents or overwrite existing KC-owned definitions. No new constituent identity or iKC ID is invented.

Final comparison: proposed `PM8-KC1-CANDIDATE` and `PM8-KC2-CANDIDATE` versus §3.1.1 `PM8-DIT-1`, §3.1.2 none, and §3.2 `PM8-SOP-1/2/3`: **unresolved scientific applicability**, with no public KC disclosure. A shared upstream resource relationship is a different biological job from reducing adipose load or testing a dietary formulation; the new pattern does not establish either pool. No duplicated public pool or delivery-only requirement was added. Existing provenance and pending gaps are retained.
