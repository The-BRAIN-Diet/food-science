## Targeted retrieval

Question: does ingested dietary protein release an indispensable amino acid into the human circulation, and what changes that appearance? Stopped at Gorissen et al. (2020), a pool of 18 trials and 602 participants: 50% ± 14% of dietary protein-derived phenylalanine appeared over five hours; casein, whey and milk differed; appearance rose with dose; older adults showed a slightly smaller fraction than young adults. No further search. The paper does not measure tryptophan or brain transmitters, and that limit is stated on the page.
