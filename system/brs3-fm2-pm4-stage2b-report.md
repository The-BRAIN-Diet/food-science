## Direct requirements and biochemical participants

The same input, input type and biological role must not be printed in both §3.1.1 and §3.1.2. Enzyme membership alone is a biochemical relationship. A human deficiency-to-enzyme result can support a dietary requirement for that mineral, bounded to low status.

| Candidate | Decision | Home | Ceiling |
