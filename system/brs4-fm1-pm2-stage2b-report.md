## Practices

Gentle cooking, iron paired with vitamin C, and whole-grain preparation were removed from System Optimisation Practices (canonical §3.2, public 3.1). No assessed study tied those practices to NAD⁺ availability.

Sleep timing was not admitted. Ramsey and Nakahata show clock control of NAMPT in mice and cells. Spiegel measured glucose tolerance after sleep debt and did not measure NAD⁺. Public 3.2 states that a regular sleep schedule has not been shown to change NAD⁺ availability in people.

Aerobic or resistance training is a lifestyle priority (canonical §3.3, public 3.2). de Guia et al. (2019) measured a rise in skeletal-muscle NAMPT protein, not NAD⁺ concentration.
