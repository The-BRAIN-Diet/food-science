mission: >-
  Maintain balanced methionine-cycle flux so methionine availability,
  SAM-dependent methylation, homocysteine recycling and transsulfuration remain
  coordinated across changing physiological demand.

summary: >-
  The methionine cycle forms S-adenosylmethionine from methionine, transfers its
  methyl group, and hydrolyses S-adenosylhomocysteine to adenosine and
  homocysteine. Homocysteine is then remethylated to methionine or sent into
  transsulfuration toward cysteine. SAM, SAH and methyl-tetrahydrofolate
  influence that split. Every tissue can remethylate homocysteine, while
  transsulfuration is limited to liver, kidney, small intestine and pancreas.
  Metabolite concentrations describe pool state, not the branch rates.

dose_sensitivity: >-

  cycle. No ordinary-diet dose-response for human remethylation or
  transsulfuration rates is established.

scientific_findings_intro: >-
  A controlled human tracer study found unchanged mean postprandial
  remethylation, transmethylation and transsulfuration rates during moderate
  vitamin B6 restriction, despite sharply reduced plasma PLP and increased
  cystathionine concentration. [[13]](#pm-ref-13) Pathway reviews provide the
  regulatory context for these measured branch rates. [[2]](#pm-ref-2)
  [[12]](#pm-ref-12) The combined evidence shows why an altered intermediate
  concentration cannot be treated as an equivalent change in overall cycle flux;
  individual responses and the restriction conditions limit generalisation.
scientific_findings:
  - id: PM4-F1
    finding_label: Homocysteine is split between remethylation and transsulfuration
    presentation: mechanistic-basis
    fm_rollup: true
    finding_summary: >-
      Methionine becomes SAM, SAM donates a methyl group, and SAH is hydrolysed
      to homocysteine. Homocysteine is then either remethylated to methionine or
      sent into transsulfuration toward cysteine.
    finding_interpretation: >-
      This is the integrated route and the branch. It does not assign the MAT,
      remethylation or transsulfuration reactions to this mechanism, and it does
      not show that ordinary diet changes the rates.
    finding_statement: >-
      Methionine-cycle throughput runs from methionine to SAM, methyl transfer
      and SAH hydrolysis to homocysteine, after which homocysteine is
      distributed between remethylation and transsulfuration under the influence
      of SAM, SAH and methyl-tetrahydrofolate.
    synthesised_evidence_confidence: not-yet-scored
    synthesis: >-
      A methylation review establishes SAM as the methyl donor and SAH
      hydrolysis to adenosine and homocysteine, and it shows that blocking that

### Overview

The methionine cycle forms S-adenosylmethionine from methionine, transfers its methyl group, and hydrolyses S-adenosylhomocysteine to adenosine and homocysteine. [[2]](#pm-ref-2) Homocysteine is then remethylated to methionine or sent into transsulfuration toward cysteine. [[12]](#pm-ref-12) SAM, SAH and methyl-tetrahydrofolate influence that split. [[12]](#pm-ref-12) Every tissue can remethylate homocysteine, while transsulfuration is limited to liver, kidney, small intestine and pancreas. [[12]](#pm-ref-12) Metabolite concentrations describe pool state, not the branch rates. [[13]](#pm-ref-13)

- **Benefits:** Maintaining methionine-cycle flux lets cells keep donating methyl groups while directing sulfur toward cysteine. [[2]](#pm-ref-2) [[12]](#pm-ref-12) That coordination helps keep methylation and sulfur exit together.
- **Implementation Notes:** Methionine, dietary protein, vitamin B9 (folate), vitamin B12 (cobalamin), vitamin B6, and vitamin B2 (riboflavin) are the provision routes. [[6]](#pm-ref-6) [[13]](#pm-ref-13) Folic acid and vitamin B12 can lower blood homocysteine, and moderate vitamin B6 restriction can raise cystathionine, without a shown change in those branch rates. [[6]](#pm-ref-6) [[13]](#pm-ref-13)
- **Biological Relevance:** [SAMe formation](/docs/biological-targets/brs2/fm1/brs2-fm1-pm3-same-synthesis), each remethylation reaction, and the [transsulfuration enzyme steps](/docs/biological-targets/brs2/fm2/brs2-fm2-pm5-transsulfuration-pathway) are neighbouring mechanisms. [[12]](#pm-ref-12)

## 2. Primary Biological Effects

↑ coordinated throughput from methionine through SAM to homocysteine<br />
↔ distribution of homocysteine between remethylation and transsulfuration<br />

## 4. Mechanistic Basis

### Summary

Methyl-group use and sulfur exit meet at homocysteine. What matters is how that intermediate is divided between return to methionine and transsulfuration, with SAM, SAH and methyl-tetrahydrofolate as effectors of the split.

<div class="brs-fm-hub-item" data-brs-fm-hub>
<div class="brs-fm-hub-shell">
<button type="button" class="brs-fm-hub-summary" aria-expanded="false">
<span class="brs-fm-hub-chevron" aria-hidden="true"></span>
<strong>Methionine cycle flux — mechanistic detail</strong>
</button>
<div class="brs-fm-hub-panel" hidden>

#### (From methionine to homocysteine)

Methionine is converted to S-adenosylmethionine (SAM), the principal methyl donor. After the methyl group is transferred, S-adenosylhomocysteine (SAH) is hydrolysed to adenosine and homocysteine. Blocking that hydrolysis can affect methylation of phospholipids, proteins, DNA, RNA and small molecules together [Chiang et al. (1996) [[2]](#pm-ref-2)].

#### (The regulated split)

Every tissue can remethylate homocysteine back to methionine. Transsulfuration, which converts homocysteine to cystathionine and onward toward cysteine, occurs in liver, kidney, small intestine and pancreas. Regulation is the distribution of homocysteine between those exits. SAM, SAH and methyl-tetrahydrofolate are effectors of that distribution [Finkelstein (1998) [[12]](#pm-ref-12)]. Human postprandial studies can measure the same partition as remethylation, transmethylation and total transsulfuration rates [Lamers et al. (2011) [[13]](#pm-ref-13)].

#### (Concentrations and rates)

Folic acid lowers blood homocysteine concentration, and vitamin B12 adds a further concentration reduction, while added vitamin B6 does not [Collaboration (1998) [[6]](#pm-ref-6)]. A small trial also reported lower plasma homocysteine after vitamin B12, fish oil, or both [Huang et al. (2015) [[11]](#pm-ref-11)]. In the tracer study, cystathionine concentration rose during vitamin B6 restriction while the mean total transsulfuration rate did not [[13]](#pm-ref-13). A concentration change is a different result from a branch rate.

#### (Boundaries of the mechanism)

This mechanism is the integrated throughput and the split at homocysteine. Formation of SAM is [BRS2-FM1-PM3 — SAMe Synthesis](/docs/biological-targets/brs2/fm1/brs2-fm1-pm3-same-synthesis). Folate- and vitamin B12-dependent remethylation is [BRS2-FM1-PM1 — Folate/B12-Dependent Homocysteine Remethylation](/docs/biological-targets/brs2/fm1/brs2-fm1-pm1-folate-b12-dependent-homocysteine-remethylation). Betaine-dependent remethylation is [BRS2-FM1-PM2 — Betaine/BHMT Remethylation](/docs/biological-targets/brs2/fm1/brs2-fm1-pm2-betaine-bhmt-remethylation). The transsulfuration reactions are [BRS2-FM2-PM5 — Transsulfuration Pathway](/docs/biological-targets/brs2/fm2/brs2-fm2-pm5-transsulfuration-pathway). Methionine-restriction experiments in ageing models, and homocysteine associations with dementia or ADHD, do not show that changing these rates treats a cognitive or attention disorder [Fanti et al. (2026) [[3]](#pm-ref-3); Luzzi et al. (2022) [[8]](#pm-ref-8); Lukovac et al. (2024) [[9]](#pm-ref-9)].

#### (Integration within methylation cycle efficiency)

SAM synthesis feeds this throughput. Remethylation returns homocysteine to methionine, and transsulfuration is the catabolic exit. Downstream methyl acceptors include phospholipids, proteins, DNA and RNA [Chiang et al. (1996) [[2]](#pm-ref-2)]. The parent account of methylation cycle efficiency is [BRS2(FM1) — Methylation Cycle Efficiency](/docs/biological-targets/brs2/fm1/brs2-fm1-methylation-cycle-efficiency).

### 4.1 Scientific Findings

#### Summary

A controlled human tracer study found unchanged mean postprandial remethylation, transmethylation and transsulfuration rates during moderate vitamin B6 restriction, despite sharply reduced plasma PLP and increased cystathionine concentration. [[13]](#pm-ref-13) Pathway reviews provide the regulatory context for these measured branch rates. [[2]](#pm-ref-2) [[12]](#pm-ref-12) The combined evidence shows why an altered intermediate concentration cannot be treated as an equivalent change in overall cycle flux; individual responses and the restriction conditions limit generalisation.

### 5.3 Local BRS Mechanism Relationships

Related Primary Mechanisms within the same Biological Regulatory System that collectively support the integrated biological function.

- [BRS2-FM1-PM1 — Folate/B12-Dependent Homocysteine Remethylation](/docs/biological-targets/brs2/fm1/brs2-fm1-pm1-folate-b12-dependent-homocysteine-remethylation) — Remethylates homocysteine to methionine and thereby carries one recycling exit. The split between that exit and transsulfuration is the upstream allocation.
- [BRS2-FM1-PM2 — Betaine/BHMT Remethylation](/docs/biological-targets/brs2/fm1/brs2-fm1-pm2-betaine-bhmt-remethylation) — Remethylates homocysteine to methionine by a betaine-dependent route that runs parallel to folate-dependent recycling and stays separate from transsulfuration.
- [BRS2-FM1-PM3 — SAMe Synthesis](/docs/biological-targets/brs2/fm1/brs2-fm1-pm3-same-synthesis) — Produces SAM from methionine and ATP. That SAM is the methyl donor consumed as throughput moves from methyl transfer to homocysteine and the branch.
- [BRS2-FM2-PM5 — Transsulfuration Pathway](/docs/biological-targets/brs2/fm2/brs2-fm2-pm5-transsulfuration-pathway) — Carries homocysteine onward to cystathionine and cysteine after the branch has directed it into transsulfuration, which is the catabolic exit rather than the recycling arm.
