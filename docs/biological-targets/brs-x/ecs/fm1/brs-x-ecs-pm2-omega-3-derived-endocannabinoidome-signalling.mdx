summary: >-
  EPA and DHA can supply the fatty-acid portions of omega-3-derived signalling lipids. DHA conversion into
  synaptamide has been traced in neuronal cells, while controlled exposures change related ethanolamides in
  human plasma. This gives a specific precursor-supply role to long-chain omega-3 inputs. It does not make all
  omega-3 molecules interchangeable: ALA conversion, delivery form, tissue metabolism and degradation affect
  the route. Neural production and clinical responses must be distinguished from circulating lipid
  measurements.

## BRS-X-ECS-1-2 - Omega-3-Derived Endocannabinoidome Signalling

## 1. Mission & Overview

### Mission

Sustain omega-3-derived ethanolamide production so endocannabinoidome neuromodulation stays supplied from dietary fat status.

### Overview

EPA and DHA can supply the fatty-acid portions of omega-3-derived signalling lipids. DHA conversion into synaptamide has been traced in neuronal cells, while controlled exposures change related ethanolamides in human plasma. This gives a specific precursor-supply role to long-chain omega-3 inputs. It does not make all omega-3 molecules interchangeable: ALA conversion, delivery form, tissue metabolism and degradation affect the route. Neural production and clinical responses must be distinguished from circulating lipid measurements.  Kevala et al. (2020) [[4]](#pm-ref-4); Pu et al. (2016) [[5]](#pm-ref-5); Yang et al. (2019) [[6]](#pm-ref-6)

- **Biological Relevance:** Both exposures supplied N-docosahexaenoyl-PE and synaptamide; equimolar free DHA generated more synaptamide.  Kevala et al. (2020) [[4]](#pm-ref-4)
- **Implementation Notes:** Use the bounded precursor-supply relationship below; its context and limitations travel with the disclosure.

## 2. Primary Biological Effects

- Both exposures supplied N-docosahexaenoyl-PE and synaptamide; equimolar free DHA generated more synaptamide.  Kevala et al. (2020) [[4]](#pm-ref-4)
- DHA-enriched oil increased its corresponding plasma ethanolamide. EPA-containing arms had higher plasma EPEA than DHA alone; combined exposure also changed DHEA.  Pu et al. (2016) [[5]](#pm-ref-5); Yang et al. (2019) [[6]](#pm-ref-6)
