## BRS-X(Hormones-PM6) - Androgen-Microbiome Regulation

## 1. Mission & Overview

### Mission

Shape systemic androgen exposure through microbiome-mediated androgen metabolism and recycling.

### Overview

The gut microbiota can alter androgens within intestinal contents by releasing hormones from conjugated forms. Mouse comparisons and human stool measurements support this local metabolic job. They do not establish how much hormone returns to circulation or changes neural androgen signalling. The relationship also runs in the other direction: hormone exposures can change microbial communities. Keeping these directions separate prevents a generic fibre or microbiome intervention from being treated as an established way to regulate systemic androgen availability.  Leao et al. (2025) [[1]](#pm-ref-1); Colldén et al. (2019) [[2]](#pm-ref-2)

- **Biological Relevance:** Microbiota enabled distal intestinal androgen deconjugation; local free DHT was high.  Colldén et al. (2019) [[2]](#pm-ref-2)

## 2. Primary Biological Effects

- Microbiota enabled distal intestinal androgen deconjugation; local free DHT was high.  Colldén et al. (2019) [[2]](#pm-ref-2)
- Hormone exposures changed microbial community and metabolic measurements.  Leao et al. (2025) [[1]](#pm-ref-1)
