## BRS-X(Hormones-PM3) - Progesterone-Supportive Microbial Metabolism

## 1. Mission & Overview

### Mission

Sustain microbial contributions to progesterone-related hormonal stability through gut ecosystem function.

### Overview

Gut bacteria can convert a host-derived biliary steroid into progestin metabolites. Defined culture and mouse experiments identify hydrogen as an important resource for this conversion. This gives the page a concrete microbial process to assess, but it does not yet establish maintenance of circulating progesterone or hormonal stability. The distinction matters for implementation: generic fibre intake or a named bacterium cannot be presented as a hormone-supporting requirement without evidence connecting that exposure to the conversion and the relevant physiological endpoint.  McCurry et al. (2024) [[2]](#pm-ref-2)

- **Biological Relevance:** Hydrogen supported conversion of 3alpha,5alpha-THDOC into tetrahydroprogesterone products; human stool observations complemented the experiments.  McCurry et al. (2024) [[2]](#pm-ref-2)

## 2. Primary Biological Effects
