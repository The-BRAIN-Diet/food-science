## BRS-X(Hormones-PM5) - Testosterone Signalling Stability

## 1. Mission & Overview

### Mission

Maintain androgen receptor signalling so motivation, stamina, and goal-directed effort remain well supported.

### Overview

Androgen signalling depends partly on the availability of hormones that activate its receptors. Nutritional and sleep conditions can influence this upstream supply, although hormone concentration is not a direct receptor-activity measurement. Small human studies found lower testosterone during zinc restriction and sleep restriction, with repletion findings confined to marginal zinc deficiency. These results support bounded supply and lifestyle relationships. They do not establish that extra zinc increases motivation or that manipulating testosterone is an appropriate goal for adequately nourished people.  Prasad et al. (1996) [[3]](#pm-ref-3); Leproult and Van Cauter (2011) [[4]](#pm-ref-4)

- **Biological Relevance:** Serum testosterone fell during restriction and rose during repletion in the small deficient groups.  Prasad et al. (1996) [[3]](#pm-ref-3)
- **Implementation Notes:** Use the bounded lifestyle relationship below; its context and limitations travel with the disclosure.

## 2. Primary Biological Effects

- Serum testosterone fell during restriction and rose during repletion in the small deficient groups.  Prasad et al. (1996) [[3]](#pm-ref-3)
- Daytime testosterone was approximately 10–15% lower after restricted sleep; reported vigour also fell.  Leproult and Van Cauter (2011) [[4]](#pm-ref-4)
