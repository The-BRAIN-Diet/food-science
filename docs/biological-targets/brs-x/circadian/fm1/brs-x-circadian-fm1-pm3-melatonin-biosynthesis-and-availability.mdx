## BRS-X-Circadian-1-3 - Melatonin Biosynthesis & Availability

## 1. Mission & Overview

### Mission

Sustain melatonin biosynthetic capacity from serotonin through AANAT-mediated acetylation and ASMT-mediated methylation. The operation is the formation of melatonin. It is distinct from light-driven circadian secretion and from melatonin receptor signalling. A change in biosynthetic capacity is not a sleep or ADHD outcome.

### Overview

Serotonin is acetylated to N-acetylserotonin by serotonin N-acetyltransferase (AANAT), using acetyl-CoA. N-acetylserotonin is methylated to melatonin by acetylserotonin O-methyltransferase (ASMT), using S-adenosylmethionine. Those reactions are the biochemical identity of this PM. [[1]](#pm-ref-1)

In eight healthy volunteers, acute tryptophan depletion lowered nocturnal melatonin AUC compared with sham depletion. Free and total plasma tryptophan fell below 20% of baseline. That supports precursor availability as a constraint. It does not show that additional dietary tryptophan raises melatonin, improves sleep or changes ADHD outcomes. [[2]](#pm-ref-2)

## 2. Primary Biological Effects

- AANAT acetylates serotonin to N-acetylserotonin. ASMT methylates N-acetylserotonin to melatonin. [[1]](#pm-ref-1)
- Acute tryptophan depletion reduced nocturnal melatonin AUC in all eight volunteers. [[2]](#pm-ref-2)
- The depletion result does not establish a benefit from extra tryptophan, better sleep or an ADHD outcome. [[2]](#pm-ref-2)

## 4. Mechanistic Basis

### Summary

Melatonin formation from serotonin uses two enzyme steps. Acetyl-CoA and SAM are the biochemical donors. A human depletion experiment shows that driving tryptophan very low reduces nocturnal melatonin secretion. Ordinary food records for tryptophan are reused and are not a new intake target. [[1]](#pm-ref-1) [[2]](#pm-ref-2)

**Mechanism boundary:** Circadian PM1 owns light effects on the circulating melatonin signal. Circadian PM2 owns feeding time. BRS1 owns tryptophan-to-serotonin signalling. BRS2 owns SAM synthesis. Exogenous melatonin capsules are outside this PM.

### 4.1 Scientific Findings

[Scientific Findings and their source assessments are available in Evidence & Audit](?review=1#scientific-findings).

The provisional Scientific Evidence Confidence for the depletion comparison is 4/8 (D2, B1, P1, R0). It is pinned to the depletion proposition only. It is not an intervention score and it is not copied onto the tryptophan relationship.
