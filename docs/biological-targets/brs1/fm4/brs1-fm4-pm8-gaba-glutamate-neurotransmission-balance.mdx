## BRS1-FM4-PM8 - GABA–Glutamate Neurotransmission Balance

(Excitation–Inhibition Balance for Neural Stability)

## 1. Mission & Overview + Dietary Levers

### Mission

Keep excitatory glutamate and inhibitory GABA signalling matched so neural networks operate with stability.


This mechanism is the integrative match between glutamate drive and GABA restraint. Magnesium, zinc, vitamin B6 and protein are not Dietary Requirements of that integrative job; they belong with sibling chemistry or remain unassessed. Dietary addressability is not established.

### Overview

Excitation–inhibition balance is the circuit-level matching of glutamatergic drive with GABAergic restraint, not the amount of either transmitter considered alone Mamiya et al. (2021) [[4]](#pm-ref-4). This mechanism integrates how the two signalling arms shape network excitability during changing functional demand, after their separate synthesis and clearance processes. Human spectroscopy can describe regional GABA or glutamate/glutamine pools associated with attention, but those concentrations do not directly measure synthesis, clearance or the dynamic balance itself Edden et al. (2012) [[1]](#pm-ref-1); Maltezos et al. (2014) [[3]](#pm-ref-3).

* **Mechanism boundary:** Covers circuit-level matching of excitation and inhibition; GABA synthesis, glutamate clearance and injury-level excitotoxicity are separate mechanisms.
* **Evidence boundary:** Spectroscopic GABA and glutamate/glutamine values are regional concentration measures, not direct measurements of reaction flux or network balance Edden et al. (2012) [[1]](#pm-ref-1); Maltezos et al. (2014) [[3]](#pm-ref-3).
* **Biological relevance:** Stable matching is relevant to attention and emotional control, but cohort associations do not establish that diet resets this balance or treats ADHD Mamiya et al. (2021) [[4]](#pm-ref-4).

## 4. Mechanistic Basis

### Summary

Matched glutamate drive and GABA restraint keep circuit excitability appropriate to the task. Sibling mechanisms cover how GABA is made, how glutamate is cleared, and how excitotoxic pressure is buffered. Imaging that reports concentration is not a measure of those rates.

#### (Excitatory–inhibitory balance as a network property)

Attention and behavioural control depend on the relative match of excitatory glutamatergic drive and inhibitory GABAergic tone. That match is a network property rather than an isolated transmitter effect → [Mamiya et al., 2021]

#### (Boundaries of the mechanism)

GABA synthesis capacity, glutamate clearance and recycling, and excitotoxic buffering are coordinated sibling jobs. Spectroscopy of GABA or glutamate/glutamine reports a pool, not those rates → [Edden et al., 2012] [Maltezos et al., 2014]

#### (Integration)

This mechanism holds the integrative balance point. Cross-system inflammation, energy supply and glycaemia can change context for networks; they are not shown here as controllers of the balance itself.

#### Introduction/Summary

Excitation–inhibition balance is a network property: how well glutamate drive and GABA restraint stay matched. The findings below separate that integrative job from GABA synthesis, glutamate clearance, and imaging concentration. They do not show that changing diet resets network balance.
