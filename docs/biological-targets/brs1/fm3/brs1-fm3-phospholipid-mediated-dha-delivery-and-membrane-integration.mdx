## BRS1(FM3) - Phospholipid-mediated DHA Delivery and Membrane Integration

(Neuronal Membrane Structure & Omega-3 Delivery)

<span id="41-core-primary-mechanisms" />

### Primary Mechanisms

- [BRS1-FM3-PM7 — Neuronal Membrane DHA Incorporation](/docs/biological-targets/brs1/fm3/brs1-fm3-pm7-neuronal-membrane-dha-incorporation)

## 1. Mission & Overview

### Mission

Maintain DHA delivery and incorporation as one contribution to the neuronal membrane environment required for signalling.

### Overview

Neuronal membranes provide the lipid environment in which receptors, channels and synapses operate. This FM currently focuses on DHA delivery and incorporation as one contribution to that environment; other membrane lipids and local maintenance processes also matter. Evidence for incorporation does not by itself establish that more dietary DHA improves cognition.

* **Biological job:** Maintain DHA's contribution to neuronal-membrane phospholipids McNamara and Carlson (2006) [\[1\]](#fm-ref-1).
* **Functional relevance:** Explain one part of the membrane environment used in neuronal communication; this page does not model all determinants of membrane composition, fluidity or structural integrity.
* **Evidence boundary:** Delivery form can affect DHA accretion in neonatal piglets; that does not establish adult human dietary superiority or improved cognition Liu et al. (2014) [\[5\]](#fm-ref-5).

## 2. Primary Biological Effects

DHA delivery → incorporation into neuronal phospholipids → contribution to the membrane environment for signalling

## 4. Mechanistic Basis (Integrated FM Narrative)

<span id="42-integrated-functional-narrative" />

### 4.1 Functional Rationale

Maintaining DHA incorporation supports neuronal membrane composition and the structural context for signalling. Current coverage is DHA-focused, rather than a comprehensive account of brain nutrient or membrane-lipid pools.

<span id="44-evidence-highlights" />

### 4.2 Evidence Summary

Human PET and plasma measurements estimated whole-brain DHA incorporation at 3.8 ± 1.7 mg/day. Turnover estimates additionally used a previously reported pool and steady-state assumptions. A porcine comparison supports delivery-form differences in gray-matter accretion, alongside reviewed carrier biology. [[4](#fm-ref-4), [5](#fm-ref-5), [6](#fm-ref-6)]

Together, these findings support delivery and replacement as contributors to maintaining membrane DHA. They do not establish an optimal oral dose, capsule-to-brain transfer fraction or cognitive benefit. The single child PM supports this bounded capacity, not comprehensive membrane integration.

### 4.3 Suboptimal Function & Its Effects

When DHA delivery or incorporation is constrained, its contribution to neuronal phospholipid composition may be reduced. Altered membrane composition can affect the environment in which signalling proteins operate, but this review does not establish a uniform change in fluidity, a whole-brain failure state or a particular cognitive consequence McNamara and Carlson (2006) [\[1\]](#fm-ref-1).

The consequences depend on the affected tissue, developmental stage and wider membrane composition. DHA incorporation is one part of membrane maintenance; this FM does not attribute all loss of structural integrity or signalling function to inadequate DHA, nor infer that increasing intake reverses those changes.
