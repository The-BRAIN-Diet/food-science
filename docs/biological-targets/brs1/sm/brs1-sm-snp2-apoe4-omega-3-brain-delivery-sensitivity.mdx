## BRS1(SM-SNP2) - APOE4 Omega-3 Brain Delivery Sensitivity

(Genetic Sensitivity to Brain Omega-3 Delivery)

## 1. Mission & Overview

### Mission

Interpret how APOE4 carriage may alter expected brain omega-3 delivery without changing underlying membrane biology.

### Overview

Human supplementation studies measure genotype-associated variation in CSF omega-3 responses. Results depend on the fatty acid, population and endpoint; a larger trial did not detect an APOE4 difference in the CSF DHA/AA response.

* The 2020 trial reported a larger CSF EPA increase in non-carriers; the DHA genotype difference was not statistically significant [Arellanes et al. (2020)](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#arellanes_brain_2020).
* The larger 2026 trial detected no APOE4 interaction for the CSF DHA/AA response [Yassine et al. (2026)](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#yassine_cns_target_2026).
* CSF exposure does not directly measure membrane incorporation, and neither trial demonstrated cognitive benefit [Arellanes et al. (2020)](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#arellanes_brain_2020), [Yassine et al. (2026)](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#yassine_cns_target_2026).

## 2. Primary Biological Effects

↑ awareness of delivery-form and genotype context in omega-3 trials; ↑ interpretation of phospholipid-bound DHA patterns within [BRS1(FM3)](/docs/biological-targets/brs1/fm3/brs1-fm3-phospholipid-mediated-dha-delivery-and-membrane-integration); ↓ over-reading population-average DHA trial results in mixed-genotype cohorts

## 4. Mechanistic Basis

### Summary

CSF fatty-acid concentrations are indicators of CNS exposure, not direct measurements of neuronal membrane incorporation or uptake flux. [Arellanes et al. (2020)](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#arellanes_brain_2020) reported a genotype difference in the CSF EPA response after DHA supplementation, whereas [Yassine et al. (2026)](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#yassine_cns_target_2026) did not detect a genotype interaction for CSF DHA/AA. Neither trial demonstrated cognitive benefit. Membrane incorporation remains the operation described by [BRS1-FM3-PM7](/docs/biological-targets/brs1/fm3/brs1-fm3-pm7-neuronal-membrane-dha-incorporation).

<strong>APOE4 context, brain DHA delivery, and trial interpretation</strong>

<div class="brs-fm-hub-panel" hidden>

#### (CSF omega-3 response — Arellanes 2020)

[Arellanes et al. (2020)](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#arellanes_brain_2020) randomised 33 cognitively unimpaired adults at risk of dementia to 2,152 mg/day triglyceride-based DHA or placebo for six months; both groups received B vitamins. Twenty-six completed paired lumbar punctures and MRI. Compared with placebo, CSF DHA increased by 28% and CSF EPA by 43%. The CSF EPA increase was approximately threefold greater in APOE4 non-carriers than carriers; this was not a threefold DHA uptake result. CSF DHA showed a non-significant trend towards a greater increase in non-carriers (p = 0.08); its treatment-by-genotype interaction was not significant (p = 0.61). The treatment-by-genotype interaction for CSF EPA was also not significant (p = 0.54). Brain volumes and cognitive scores did not differ between treatment groups.

#### (CSF DHA/AA response — Yassine 2026)

[Yassine et al. (2026)](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#yassine_cns_target_2026) randomised 365 adults aged 55–80 years without dementia and with low dietary DHA intake to 2 g/day DHA or placebo for 24 months. Among 167 participants with CSF data, the six-month change in CSF DHA/arachidonic-acid ratio was +0.17 with DHA versus −0.02 with placebo (between-group difference 0.19, 95% CI 0.16–0.21). No treatment-by-APOE4 interaction was detected (p = 0.71). Brain volumes and cognitive performance did not differ between treatment groups over 24 months. The trial had 38% dropout, mainly related to COVID-19.

#### (APOE4 frequency in a defined Alzheimer’s cohort)

[Quinn et al. (2010)](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#quinn_dha_cognitive_2010) enrolled 402 people with mild-to-moderate Alzheimer’s disease; 232 (57.7%) carried APOE ε4. This is the genotype distribution of that trial, not a universal prevalence or a 40% upper bound. DHA at 2 g/day for 18 months did not slow cognitive or functional decline overall. Exploratory non-carrier subgroup findings do not establish a general genotype-specific treatment benefit.

#### (Carrier-form and endpoint boundaries)

Arellanes tested triglyceride-based DHA, not a comparison of phosphatidylcholine-DHA, LPC-DHA or whole foods. Patrick’s transport discussion is secondary synthesis, not evidence that these foods or formulations overcome APOE4-associated differences → [Patrick (2019)](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#patrick_role_2019). CSF enrichment, membrane incorporation and cognitive outcomes are distinct endpoints.
