title: ROS Generation vs Clearance Balance

mission: Balance reactive oxygen species production and clearance so net oxidative pressure stays within tolerable limits.

summary: 'Cells generate reactive oxygen species (ROS) during ordinary immune and metabolic activity, and enzymatic clearance uses selenium, zinc, copper, and manganese. Net oxidative pressure is the balance of generation and clearance, not the level of one marker. When generation outruns clearance, oxidative damage accumulates across membranes, proteins, and mitochondria. High-heat cooking can add advanced glycation end products that raise this pressure.'

  - id: PM4-F1
    finding_label: Three enzymes clear superoxide and hydrogen peroxide
    presentation: mechanistic-basis
    fm_rollup: true
    finding_summary: Superoxide dismutase converts superoxide to hydrogen peroxide. Catalase and glutathione peroxidase convert hydrogen peroxide to water. Copper-zinc superoxide dismutase holds copper and zinc. Mitochondrial superoxide dismutase holds manganese. Glutathione peroxidase is a selenoprotein and uses glutathione.
    finding_interpretation: 'Enzyme metals and the peroxidase thiol are biochemical participants. They are not, by themselves, proof that eating more of them changes the balance.'
    finding_statement: 'Mammalian cells clear superoxide and hydrogen peroxide with superoxide dismutase, catalase and glutathione peroxidase. Copper, zinc, manganese, selenium and glutathione belong to those enzymes. That membership is not an intake result.'
    synthesised_evidence_confidence: not-yet-scored
    synthesis: 'Weydert and Cullen describe the three primary antioxidant enzymes, the copper-zinc and manganese superoxide dismutases, catalase, and glutathione peroxidase as a selenoprotein that requires glutathione. ### Overview

Cells generate reactive oxygen species (ROS) during ordinary immune and metabolic activity, and enzymatic clearance uses selenium, zinc, copper, and manganese. [[21]](#pm-ref-21) Net oxidative pressure is the balance of generation and clearance, not the level of one marker. [[21]](#pm-ref-21) When generation outruns clearance, oxidative damage accumulates across membranes, proteins, and mitochondria. [[21]](#pm-ref-21) High-heat cooking can add advanced glycation end products that raise this pressure. [[5]](#pm-ref-5)

- **Benefits:** Balancing production against clearance helps keep net oxidative pressure within what cells can tolerate. [[21]](#pm-ref-21) When production outruns clearance, the excess reaches membranes, proteins, and mitochondria. [[21]](#pm-ref-21)
- **Implementation Notes:** Selenium and copper are the provision routes. [[22]](#pm-ref-22) [[23]](#pm-ref-23) Glutathione is the thiol used in peroxidase clearance, not a dietary target. [[21]](#pm-ref-21) Extra intake when status is already adequate is not established. [[22]](#pm-ref-22)
- **Biological Relevance:** [Membrane lipid damage](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm2/brs3-fm2-pm5-lipid-peroxidation-control) is a later result of that pressure, not this balance. [[11]](#pm-ref-11)

## 4. Mechanistic Basis

### Summary

Net oxidative pressure is whether superoxide dismutase, catalase and glutathione peroxidase keep up with reactive oxygen made in ordinary metabolism. [[21]](#pm-ref-21) Selenium and copper are the intake routes that restore glutathione peroxidase and copper-zinc superoxide dismutase when status is low. [[22]](#pm-ref-22) [[23]](#pm-ref-23) Manganese additionally supplies MnSOD capacity; this does not establish an extra-intake benefit. Zinc and glutathione participate in distinct enzyme reactions; additional dietary supply relationships need separate evidence. [[21]](#pm-ref-21)

#### (Generation and clearance)

Cells make superoxide and hydrogen peroxide during ordinary aerobic metabolism. Superoxide dismutase converts superoxide to hydrogen peroxide. Catalase and glutathione peroxidase convert hydrogen peroxide to water. [[21]](#pm-ref-21) Copper-zinc superoxide dismutase is cytosolic. Manganese superoxide dismutase is mitochondrial. Glutathione peroxidase is a selenoprotein and uses glutathione. [[21]](#pm-ref-21)

#### (What intake has been shown to change)

In adults whose diet supplied about 14 micrograms of selenium a day, plasma glutathione peroxidase activity levelled off at a total intake of about 35 micrograms a day. More selenium did not raise that enzyme further. [[22]](#pm-ref-22) In infants recovering from malnutrition with marginal copper, copper supplementation raised red-cell superoxide dismutase. [[23]](#pm-ref-23) Neither result is a reason to add more when status is already adequate.

#### (Boundaries of the mechanism)

Catalase uses haem iron. No human study retrieved for this page shows that iron intake sets catalase activity, so iron is not a dietary requirement here. Mitochondrial production of reactive oxygen is [BRS4-FM2-PM4 — ROS Production and Control](/docs/biological-targets/brs4/fm2/brs4-fm2-pm4-ros-production-and-control). Transcriptional induction of the enzymes is [BRS3-FM2-PM3 — Nrf2-Mediated Cellular Defence Regulation](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm2/brs3-fm2-pm3-nrf2-are-antioxidant-activation). Membrane lipid damage is [BRS3-FM2-PM5 — Lipid Peroxidation Control](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm2/brs3-fm2-pm5-lipid-peroxidation-control). Recycling among network antioxidants is [BRS3-FM2-PM6 — Antioxidant Network Recycling](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm2/brs3-fm2-pm6-antioxidant-network-recycling).

#### (Integration within BRS3)

This page is the generation-versus-clearance balance inside [BRS3(FM2)](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm2/brs3-fm2-antioxidant-defense-capacity). A shared precursor-pool constraint is not established for this balance.

### 4.1 Scientific Findings

#### Summary

Net oxidative pressure is the balance of reactive-oxygen generation and enzymatic clearance. Superoxide dismutase, catalase and glutathione peroxidase do that clearance. Selenium and copper are the dietary provision routes. Manganese supplies MnSOD catalytic capacity; zinc and glutathione participate in these reactions, but this manganese evidence does not establish their dietary supply relationships. Extra intake when status is adequate is not established.
