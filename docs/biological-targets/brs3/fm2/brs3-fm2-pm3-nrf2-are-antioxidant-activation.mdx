### Overview

Nrf2 is held by Keap1 until oxidative or electrophilic stress lets it reach defence genes. Itoh et al. (1999) showed that Keap1 represses Nrf2 and that electrophiles release that repression. [[7]](#pm-ref-7) Zhang et al. (2004) showed that Keap1 marks Nrf2 for disposal until stress or sulforaphane slows that disposal. [[8]](#pm-ref-8) A three-day broccoli sprout homogenate raised phase II enzyme RNA in nasal cells. [[5]](#pm-ref-5) That is airway gene expression, not brain Nrf2, and sulforaphane is not a nutrient this regulation requires.

- **Benefits:** This regulation lets cells raise defence-gene transcription when oxidative or electrophilic stress rises. [[7]](#pm-ref-7)
- **Implementation Notes:** The tested practice is a myrosinase-completed broccoli sprout homogenate once a day for three days. [[5]](#pm-ref-5) Capsules of extracted sulforaphane did not raise the same class of genes in COPD lung cells. [[6]](#pm-ref-6)
- **Biological Relevance:** The enzymes this regulation induces are used by later clearance, membrane, and recycling steps — see PM4, PM5 and PM6.

## 2. Primary Biological Effects
Defence-gene transcription rises when stress releases Nrf2 from Keap1

## 4. Mechanistic Basis

### Summary

Cellular defence-gene expression is regulated when oxidative or electrophilic stress releases Nrf2 from Keap1. That regulation sits within [BRS3(FM2) - Antioxidant Defense Capacity](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm2/brs3-fm2-antioxidant-defense-capacity).

#### (Nrf2-ARE signalling)

Itoh et al. (1999) showed that Keap1 represses Nrf2 until electrophiles release that hold. [[7]](#pm-ref-7) Zhang et al. (2004) showed that Keap1 marks Nrf2 for disposal, and that oxidative stress or sulforaphane slows that disposal. [[8]](#pm-ref-8) Raising the response is not, by itself, the aim of this regulation.

#### (Isothiocyanate signalling and induction kinetics)

Riedl, Saxon and Diaz-Sanchez (2009) found that a three-day broccoli sprout homogenate, finished with daikon myrosinase, raised phase II enzyme RNA in nasal cells. [[5]](#pm-ref-5) Wise et al. (2016) found that four weeks of extracted sulforaphane capsules did not raise Nrf2 target genes in COPD lung cells. [[6]](#pm-ref-6) Sulforaphane is an inducer in this preparation, not a nutrient the regulation requires.

#### (Boundaries of the mechanism)

Net ROS generation versus clearance dynamics are handled by [BRS3-FM2-PM4 - ROS Generation vs Clearance Balance](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm2/brs3-fm2-pm4-ros-generation-vs-clearance-balance). Membrane lipid protection belongs to [BRS3-FM2-PM5 - Lipid Peroxidation Control](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm2/brs3-fm2-pm5-lipid-peroxidation-control). Antioxidant-network recycling and regeneration are represented by [BRS3-FM2-PM6 - Antioxidant Network Recycling](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm2/brs3-fm2-pm6-antioxidant-network-recycling).

#### (Integration within BRS3)

This PM supports endogenous defence capacity within [BRS3(FM2)](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm2/brs3-fm2-antioxidant-defense-capacity). Vitamin C, zinc and selenium are not established here as requirements of the activation step.

## 5. BRS Pathways and Connections

### 5.1 BRS Pathways

- None listed

### 5.2 Cross-BRS Mechanism Relationships

Primary Mechanisms in other Biological Regulatory Systems that directly interact with, constrain or support this mechanism.

- [BRS4-FM1-PM1 — Electron Transport Chain Function](/docs/biological-targets/brs4/fm1/brs4-fm1-pm1-electron-transport-chain-function) — Mitochondrial electron transport is a source of reactive oxygen that this regulation responds to. It is not Nrf2 transcription itself.
- [BRS4-FM2-PM4 — ROS Production and Control](/docs/biological-targets/brs4/fm2/brs4-fm2-pm4-ros-production-and-control) — Mitochondrial reactive-oxygen control is an upstream source of oxidative load. This page regulates defence-gene expression rather than generating that load.

### 5.3 Local BRS Mechanism Relationships

Related Primary Mechanisms within the same Biological Regulatory System that collectively support the integrated biological function.

- [BRS3-FM2-PM4 - ROS Generation vs Clearance Balance](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm2/brs3-fm2-pm4-ros-generation-vs-clearance-balance) — Clearance enzymes set net oxidative pressure. This page regulates expression of those defence genes. It is not the clearance balance.
- [BRS3-FM2-PM5 - Lipid Peroxidation Control](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm2/brs3-fm2-pm5-lipid-peroxidation-control) — Lipid peroxidation is membrane damage downstream of oxidative load. This regulation does not itself stop that chain reaction.
- [BRS3-FM2-PM6 - Antioxidant Network Recycling](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm2/brs3-fm2-pm6-antioxidant-network-recycling) — Recycling regenerates vitamin and thiol antioxidants after use. This page regulates defence-gene expression and does not restore the spent vitamins.
