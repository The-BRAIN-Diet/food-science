## BRS3-FM3-PM7 - Cytokine Network Modulation

(Coordinating Immune Signalling Intensity)

## 1. Mission & Overview + Dietary Levers

### Mission

Tune downstream cytokine signalling intensity so inflammatory tone does not remain chronically elevated.


**Intervention Dominance:** Not established — No principal intervention route established

No cited study establishes a preparation, supplement protocol, sleep practice, activity practice, or stress-recovery practice that changes this circulating cytokine step. Omega-3 capsules were tested in two attached trials. One did not change plasma IL-6, TNF-α, or CRP. The other lowered stimulated IL-6 production in student blood cells. Neither result is a practice for this circulating cytokine network.

### Overview

IL-6 and TNF-α are inflammatory messengers. Slavich and Irwin (2014) place them on the path from stress-related immune signalling to inflammatory tone. [[5]](#pm-ref-5) Ferguson et al. (2014) used an endotoxin challenge in healthy adults to read that response. [[2]](#pm-ref-2) EPA plus DHA did not significantly change IL-6 or TNF-α in that challenge. [[2]](#pm-ref-2) CRP is a blood marker, not the messenger step.

- **Benefits:** Tuning this intensity is about the messengers themselves, including IL-6 and TNF-α, after upstream genes have already been switched on. [[5]](#pm-ref-5)
- **Implementation Notes:** No dietary requirement is established. In the endotoxin trial, EPA plus DHA did not significantly change IL-6 or TNF-α. [[2]](#pm-ref-2)
- **Biological Relevance:** The transcriptional step that sits ahead of these messengers is separate — see PM1.

## 4. Mechanistic Basis

### Summary

Inflammatory tone is expressed not only at transcriptional nodes but through circulating cytokine networks. This PM captures whether IL-6, TNF-α, and CRP-linked signalling remains elevated or moves toward resolution within [BRS3(FM3) - Inflammation Resolution Capacity](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm3/brs3-fm3-inflammation-resolution-capacity).

#### (Cytokine signalling burden)

IL-6, TNF-α, and CRP-linked pathways act as downstream readouts of inflammatory tone, reflecting how unresolved immune signalling is expressed systemically.

#### (Network modulation logic)

Cytokine pressure integrates upstream inputs from transcriptional, gut-derived, and lipid-mediator pathways. Modulation here reflects whether inflammatory signalling remains escalated or is progressively brought down—not a single receptor or cytokine in isolation.

#### (Boundaries of the mechanism)

Upstream NF-κB transcriptional control is handled by [BRS3-FM1-PM1 - NF-kB Signalling Regulation](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm1/brs3-fm1-pm1-nf-kb-signalling-regulation). Gut-derived endotoxin signalling belongs to [BRS3-FM1-PM2 - Gut-Derived Inflammatory Signalling](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm1/brs3-fm1-pm2-gut-derived-inflammatory-signalling). Lipid-mediator resolution substrate balance is represented by [BRS3-FM3-PM8 - Eicosanoid / SPM Balance](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm3/brs3-fm3-pm8-eicosanoid-spm-balance).

#### (Integration within BRS3)

This PM supports resolution-oriented inflammatory tone within [BRS3(FM3)](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm3/brs3-fm3-inflammation-resolution-capacity). EPA and DHA are substrates on the neighbouring eicosanoid page. They are not a requirement of this cytokine step.

IL-6 and TNF-α are messenger signals of inflammatory tone. CRP is a blood marker linked to that tone, not the messenger step. An endotoxin challenge in healthy adults has been used to read these responses. EPA plus DHA did not significantly change IL-6 or TNF-α in that challenge.
