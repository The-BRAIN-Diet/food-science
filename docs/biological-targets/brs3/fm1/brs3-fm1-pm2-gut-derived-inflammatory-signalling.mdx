### Overview

Lipopolysaccharide can enter the blood when the gut barrier changes. Mohammad and Thiemermann (2021) review that rise as a route to low-grade systemic inflammation through Toll-like receptor 4. [[1]](#pm-ref-1) Gut bacteria ferment dietary fibre to short-chain fatty acids, including butyrate. Koh et al. (2016) describe that fermentation. [[10]](#pm-ref-10) It was not shown to lower endotoxin translocation.

- **Benefits:** Limiting this spillover keeps microbial products from adding to systemic inflammation after the barrier has changed. [[1]](#pm-ref-1)
- **Implementation Notes:** No intake was shown to lower endotoxin translocation. Fermentable fibre and butyrate are not dietary targets for this step. [[10]](#pm-ref-10)
- **Biological Relevance:** The transcriptional response that can follow this inflammatory load is a later step — see PM1.

## 4. Mechanistic Basis

### Summary

Systemic inflammatory tone can be driven partly by gut-derived signals. When barrier integrity weakens, endotoxin and related microbial inputs enter circulation more readily, increasing low-grade inflammatory signalling within [BRS3(FM1) - Anti-Inflammatory Signalling Tone](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm1/brs3-fm1-anti-inflammatory-signalling-tone).

#### (Endotoxin-linked inflammatory load)

Mohammad and Thiemermann (2021) review lipopolysaccharide reaching the blood after a change in the gut lining, with inflammatory signalling through Toll-like receptor 4. [[1]](#pm-ref-1) That is the spillover this mechanism governs.

#### (Microbial and barrier modulation logic)

Koh et al. (2016) describe fermentation of dietary fibre to short-chain fatty acids. [[10]](#pm-ref-10) Cavaliere et al. (2022) gave butyrate to obese mice and saw a partial fall in cortical neuroinflammation. [[6]](#pm-ref-6) Neither result measures lipopolysaccharide crossing the barrier, so neither is a dietary requirement of this spillover.

#### (Boundaries of the mechanism)

Upstream NF-κB transcriptional control is handled by [BRS3-FM1-PM1 - NF-kB Signalling Regulation](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm1/brs3-fm1-pm1-nf-kb-signalling-regulation). Downstream cytokine-network expression belongs to [BRS3-FM3-PM7 - Cytokine Network Modulation](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm3/brs3-fm3-pm7-cytokine-network-modulation). Native gut-barrier biology is represented within [BRS5](/docs/biological-targets/gut-brain-axis-enteric-nervous-system); this PM captures the BRS3 inflammatory-tone output of gut-derived signals.

#### (Integration within BRS3)

This PM links gut-derived inputs to inflammatory tone within [BRS3(FM1)](/docs/biological-targets/brs3/fm1/brs3-fm1-anti-inflammatory-signalling-tone). Barrier and endotoxin-containment context imported through section 4.3 connected mechanisms conditions translocation load.
