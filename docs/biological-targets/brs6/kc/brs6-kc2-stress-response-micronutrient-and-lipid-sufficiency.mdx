### BRS6(KC2) - Stress-Response Micronutrient & Lipid Sufficiency

(Distinct micronutrient cofactors and membrane lipids; shared-pool status unresolved)

### 1. Ambition

Maintain adequate micronutrient and membrane-lipid status while keeping their distinct cofactor, oxygen-handling, structural, and signalling roles scientifically separate.

### 3. Evidence Base

#### Summary

The sources establish that micronutrients perform varied cofactor, energy-metabolism, oxygen-handling, and neuronal roles, while EPA and DHA contribute to membrane structure and signalling ([Tardy et al., 2020](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#tardy_vitamins_2020); [Kennedy, 2016](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#kennedy_b_2016); [McNamara and Carlson, 2006](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#mcnamara_role_2006)). They do not identify one interchangeable “stress-response” resource, demonstrate that availability of these nutrients jointly limits HPA-axis and autonomic recovery, or justify combining micronutrient cofactors with long-chain omega-3 membrane lipids as a single nutritional bottleneck.

- **Constraint and membership boundary:** Biochemical importance does not make magnesium, vitamin C, B vitamins, iron, zinc, EPA, and DHA members of one pool; their cited roles are materially different ([Tardy et al., 2020](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#tardy_vitamins_2020); [McNamara and Carlson, 2006](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#mcnamara_role_2006)).
- **Evidence and measurement boundary:** The reviews describe nutrient functions and possible deficiency consequences, but they do not measure a shared resource, joint limitation, or common dose-response across the connected mechanisms ([Tardy et al., 2020](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#tardy_vitamins_2020); [Kennedy, 2016](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#kennedy_b_2016)).
- **Biological relevance and provision limitation:** Adequacy of each nutrient may matter in its own biological context, yet these sources do not show that supplementation in replete people improves cortisol rhythm, vagal recovery, or autonomic function ([Tardy et al., 2020](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#tardy_vitamins_2020); [McNamara and Carlson, 2006](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#mcnamara_role_2006)).
