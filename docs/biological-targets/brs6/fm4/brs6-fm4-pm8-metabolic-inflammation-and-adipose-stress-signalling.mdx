    presentation: mechanistic-basis
    fm_rollup: false
    finding_summary: >-
      Human adipose cortisol regeneration is tissue- and metabolic-context dependent; whole-body dietary
      changes in cortisol metabolism do not necessarily alter subcutaneous adipose 11β-HSD1.
    finding_statement: >-
      Human adipose cortisol regeneration is tissue- and metabolic-context dependent; whole-body dietary
      changes in cortisol metabolism do not necessarily alter subcutaneous adipose 11β-HSD1.
    finding_interpretation: >-
      Microdialysis and tracer measurements distinguish local cortisone-to-cortisol conversion from systemic
      turnover. Insulin effects differed by adiposity. A low-carbohydrate crossover altered systemic cortisol
      metabolism without a measured adipose 11β-HSD1 response; weight loss in another small study increased
      adipocyte transcript expression rather than demonstrating universal suppression.
    synthesis: >-
      Microdialysis and tracer measurements distinguish local cortisone-to-cortisol conversion from systemic
      turnover. Insulin effects differed by adiposity. A low-carbohydrate crossover altered systemic cortisol
      metabolism without a measured adipose 11β-HSD1 response; weight loss in another small study increased
      adipocyte transcript expression rather than demonstrating universal suppression.
    synthesis_limitations: >-
      Small selected samples and differing assays; transcript abundance is not in vivo regeneration flux.
      Systemic or hepatic effects cannot be assigned to adipose, and less local cortisol activity is not
      assumed universally beneficial.

## BRS6-4-8 - Adipose Endocrine Regulation

(Communicating Metabolic State Through Adipose Hormones)

## 1. Mission & Overview

### Mission

Regulate adipose-derived endocrine signalling and local glucocorticoid responsiveness to communicate metabolic state and coordinate hormonal adaptation.

**Intervention Dominance:** Diet-Supported — Context-specific dietary influence is established; no principal mission-wide intervention route selected.

### Overview

Adipose tissue communicates metabolic state through hormones, including leptin and adiponectin. Production, release, circulating concentration and receptor responsiveness are distinct. Local glucocorticoid regeneration is a further tissue-specific process; systemic cortisol changes do not identify that local activity. [[1]](#pm-ref-1); [[2]](#pm-ref-2)

## 2. Primary Biological Effects

- Studied energy-restriction programmes and aerobic exercise can alter selected adipose hormone measurements; responses depend on tissue, population and protocol. [[3]](#pm-ref-3); [[4]](#pm-ref-4); [[5]](#pm-ref-5)
- Defined DHA formulations and milled-flaxseed protocols have bounded circulating adiponectin evidence, not proof of local secretion or a universal beneficial response. [[6]](#pm-ref-6); [[7]](#pm-ref-7); [[8]](#pm-ref-8) The DHA-specific result was observed with 2.7 g/day DHA for ten weeks. Whether customary dietary intakes produce a comparable response remains unknown. [[6]](#pm-ref-6)
- Administered urolithin A has preclinical adipose endocrine evidence. Human associations and a null supplementation fat-mass result limit translation to food-derived human effects. [[9]](#pm-ref-9); [[10]](#pm-ref-10); [[11]](#pm-ref-11); [[12]](#pm-ref-12)

## 4. Mechanistic Basis

### Summary

Hormone release and local cortisol regeneration let adipose tissue respond to metabolic conditions. [[1]](#pm-ref-1); [[2]](#pm-ref-2)

#### Biological process

<span id="pm8-mechanism-f13" />

Local versus systemic glucocorticoid regulation qualifies the weight-loss SOP: tissue transcript changes do not establish matching enzyme activity or cortisol flux. SF1-PM-Scope2 — Local adipose glucocorticoid regulation retains the contrasting and null findings.

#### (Boundaries of the mechanism) {#mechanism-boundary}

A blood hormone response is not a measurement of secretion rate or receptor sensitivity. Admitted relationships retain their food-form, dose, tissue and population limits; an SOP is not a food-specific Dietary Lever.

## 5. BRS Pathways and Connections

### 5.1 BRS Pathways

Peripheral endocrine communication contributes to metabolic–endocrine adaptation. No whole-body resource-allocation mechanism is inferred.

### 5.2 Cross-BRS Mechanism Relationships

BRS3 inflammatory regulation can influence adipose endocrine conditions, but inflammation is not this PM’s defining operation. No new causal graph or brain-outcome relationship is admitted.

### 5.3 Local BRS Mechanism Relationships

[Insulin Sensitivity & Glucose Disposal](/docs/biological-targets/brs6/fm1/brs6-fm1-pm3-insulin-sensitivity-and-glucose-disposal) addresses insulin-responsive disposal; [Cortisol Rhythm Regulation](/docs/biological-targets/brs6/fm2/brs6-fm2-pm4-cortisol-rhythm-regulation) addresses systemic timing. [Stress-Modulated Appetite & Reward Regulation](/docs/biological-targets/brs6/fm4/brs6-fm4-pm9-stress-induced-appetite-reward-drive-modulation) addresses central appetite/reward integration, not adipokine production.
