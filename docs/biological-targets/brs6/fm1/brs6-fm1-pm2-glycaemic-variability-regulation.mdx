title: Glycaemic Variability Regulation
pm_id: BRS6-FM1-PM2
mission: Limit glycaemic volatility so post-prandial glucose swings do not create unnecessary metabolic and neuroendocrine stress.
parent_fm: BRS6(FM1)
parent_brs: BRS6
summary: 'Blood glucose varies as meals arrive and tissues take up their fuel. Regulation concerns the size, duration and frequency of these changes, rather than keeping glucose perfectly flat. In people with type 2 diabetes, short aerobic training reduced measured excursions. The order of a meal also changed within-meal variability. These findings identify activity and meal protocols that can influence different parts of the glucose pattern.'

## BRS6-1-2 - Glycaemic Variability Regulation


## 1. Mission & Overview

### Mission

Limit glycaemic volatility so post-prandial glucose swings do not create unnecessary metabolic and neuroendocrine stress.

**Intervention Dominance:** Diet-Dominant (inherited qualification, retained pending approval). Principal route displayed: Lifestyle Levers. This placement reflects the assessed process, not proven comparative superiority.

### Overview

Blood glucose varies as meals arrive and tissues take up their fuel. Regulation concerns the size, duration and frequency of these changes, rather than keeping glucose perfectly flat. In a small uncontrolled type 2 diabetes cohort, short aerobic training was associated with selected excursion changes. The order of a meal also changed within-meal variability. These findings identify activity and meal protocols that can influence different parts of the glucose pattern. Monnier et al. (2006) [[1]](#pm-ref-1); Mikus et al. (2012) [[2]](#pm-ref-2); Kuwata et al. (2016) [[5]](#pm-ref-5); Dunstan et al. (2012) [[6]](#pm-ref-6)

- **Benefits:** Selected protocols changed measured glucose-dispersion endpoints in the studied diabetes cohorts; this does not establish a clinical or cognitive benefit. Mikus et al. (2012) [[2]](#pm-ref-2)
- **Implementation Notes:** Aerobic activity was assessed over seven days; this is not evidence that every ordinary fluctuation needs correcting. Monnier et al. (2006) [[1]](#pm-ref-1); Mikus et al. (2012) [[2]](#pm-ref-2); Kuwata et al. (2016) [[5]](#pm-ref-5); Dunstan et al. (2012) [[6]](#pm-ref-6)
- **Biological Relevance:** A smaller average glucose response does not necessarily demonstrate lower variability.

## 2. Primary Biological Effects

- Short aerobic training was associated with selected excursion changes in an uncontrolled diabetes cohort; high-excursion frequency and duration remained inconclusive. Mikus et al. (2012) [[2]](#pm-ref-2)
- Fish before rice reduced within-meal glucose variability in the diabetes group. Kuwata et al. (2016) [[5]](#pm-ref-5)
- An observational diabetes study linked glucose fluctuation measures with urinary oxidative-stress markers. Monnier et al. (2006) [[1]](#pm-ref-1)
- Brief walking interruptions reduced postprandial glucose and insulin exposure versus continuous sitting. Dunstan et al. (2012) [[6]](#pm-ref-6)
