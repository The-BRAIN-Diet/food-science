This is the web equivalent of the generated **substance-index.md** report. Both use the same canonical PM-input, substance and food records.

Search by substance or food. Use the independent relationship checkboxes and optional preparation filter. Each substance includes a quantified preview across eligible preparations, other supported sources and links to the selected PM relationships. Accounting belongs to those relationships. Quantities describe the named material and preparation; they do not establish dietary requirements or serving sizes.
