## Comparative findings

1. **Functional distinction:** §1.1.1 explains an accepted dietary provision relationship; §1.1.2 explains reaction participation and supply/regeneration. A repeated name earns its place only through different information.
2. **Biochemical-only substrate:** ATP in MAT and neuronal glutamate in GAD belong in biochemical context when the accepted evidence does not admit direct dietary supply to that reaction. Endogenous regeneration does not change substrate identity.
3. **Dietary-only precursor:** B6 can be the dietary provision entry while PLP is the reaction cofactor. B6 itself is not an additional GAD/AADC cofactor.
4. **Legitimate dual presentation:** methionine or tyrosine can appear as a supplied dietary input and a consumed reaction substrate. Iron can appear as mineral provision and a catalytic cofactor. Distinct explanations matter more than a repeated name.
5. **Unnecessary repetition:** copying the same atom's role and limitation twice adds no information. The current GABA PLP and MAT methionine presentations require distinct explanations or consolidation, not automatic deletion of biochemical context.
6. **Endogenous supply:** state explicitly that the parent includes reaction context, not just intake targets. Name endogenous synthesis, salvage or regeneration beside each participant. Do not equate redox regeneration of NAD⁺ with replenishment after NAD⁺ consumption.
7. **Single reaction versus complex PM:** the distinction is clearest for MAT and GAD. Dopamine requires a synthesis subpathway within its broader unchanged mission. NAD⁺ needs a pathway-level summary; listing every intermediate would exceed the accepted records and obscure tissue dependence. These demonstrations test the proposed interpretation; they do not prove one layout is best for all PMs.
8. **Instruction disagreements:** the same-input/type/role no-reprint rule does not provide a way to express separate provision and reaction explanations on one atom. Derived-target validation currently requires a Direct-classified target, which conflicts with treating Direct solely as independently admitted dietary provision when PLP supply is not established. Capacity requirements must not inherit regulatory/therapeutic response thresholds. The broad dietary-protein delivery admission also needs specificity review.

## Proposed replacement wording — not implemented

> §1.1.1 identifies independently accepted dietary provision or regulatory relationships. Preserve Direct/Derived classification and name the supported route. Biochemical participation alone does not establish dietary admission. Nutritional sufficiency does not require demonstrated benefit from increasing an already adequate intake.

> §1.1.2 identifies reaction participants and explains their step, active form, principal supply/regeneration and connection to dietary provision. A substance may appear in both sections when provision and reaction function add different information. Cross-reference shared records; do not create another supply or duplicate an explanation. Do not require a complete intermediate inventory where the accepted PM evidence supports only a pathway summary.

> A Derived dietary route may name its supported biochemical target without implying that the target has a separate direct-intake requirement. Preserve precursor/target identities and conversion evidence. Reconcile the current Direct-target validator before changing canonical classifications.

> §1.1.3 retains accepted shared-pool and upstream relationships, including ownership and limitations. It does not automatically promote constituents to dietary requirements. SOPs require their own accepted intervention evidence, not merely nutritional necessity. Preserve the existing regulatory-modulator rule pending separate review.

## Minimal implementation choices for review

- Prefer separate provision and reaction explanations referencing existing canonical records, rather than new requirement classes.
- Decide whether active targets such as PLP remain explanatory targets under a Derived entry or receive a separate biochemical entry; their independent dietary admission must be judged separately.
- Decide whether NAD⁺ needs finer reaction-specific evidence before expanding its biochemical inventory.
- Preserve stable source IDs and supply ownership; calculate each dietary analyte once regardless of presentation or PM count.
- This pilot uses existing Markdown/MDX, admonitions, tables and Mermaid; no shared component changes were needed. It does not mount canonical PM enhancer/food components because that would project the old placement rules into the isolated experiment.
