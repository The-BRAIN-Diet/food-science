## Overview

Methionine adenosyltransferase uses methionine and ATP to form SAM. PEMT and GAMT use its methyl group in phosphatidylcholine and creatine synthesis [1,2].

## Dietary Context

Dietary methionine can contribute to endogenous SAM supply. Methyl-donor availability, competing reactions, tissue compartmentation and feedback remain distinct from a recommendation to consume SAM or a claim of benefit above adequacy [1,2].
