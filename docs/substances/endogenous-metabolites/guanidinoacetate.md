## Overview

AGAT transfers an amidino group from arginine to glycine, forming guanidinoacetate; GAMT uses a SAM methyl group to produce creatine. Human renal arteriovenous measurements establish GAA production, separately from cerebral synthesis or dietary intervention efficacy [1,2].

## Dietary Context

This is an endogenous conversion node. Qualified upstream arginine/glycine provision does not imply foods contain GAA or provide a fixed creatine yield. Precursor supply, organ exchange, feedback regulation and tissue transport remain distinct [1,2].
