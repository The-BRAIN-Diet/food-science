Fructans are fructose polymers. Inulin-type fructans are the linear β(2→1) members. Oligofructose is the short-chain mixture in that family. Longer-chain inulin, oligofructose-enriched inulin, and sucrose-derived fructooligosaccharides are related names, not the same substance.
