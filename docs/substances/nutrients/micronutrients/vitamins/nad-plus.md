## Overview

Oxidised NAD is formed from beta-NMN and ATP by NMN adenylyltransferase [1]. Its redox state, cellular compartment and free or protein-associated pools must be distinguished.

## Dietary Context

Dietary precursors can support NAD supply. A precursor amount is not an amount of NAD formed, and a blood response does not demonstrate delivery into every tissue or mitochondrial compartment. No direct food NAD-content edge is created here.
