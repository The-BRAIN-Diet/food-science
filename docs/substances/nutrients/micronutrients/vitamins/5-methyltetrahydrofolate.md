## Overview

5-Methyltetrahydrofolate, also called 5-MTHF, 5-methyl-THF or L-methylfolate, is a reduced form of [vitamin B9 (folate)](/docs/substances/nutrients/micronutrients/vitamins/vitamin-b9). In vegetables it is often one of the principal naturally occurring folate forms, commonly as polyglutamates rather than as folic acid, the synthetic monoglutamate used in fortification [1]. The structure shown is the (6S) isomer. This page records that form. A total-folate figure remains a family measurement.

Methionine synthase transfers the methyl group of 5-MTHF to homocysteine, forming methionine and tetrahydrofolate [2]. Other dietary folate forms can reach the same molecule through folate metabolism, including the MTHFR-dependent step [2]. That role describes the reaction. It does not show that a higher intake increases tissue enzyme flux, and an L-methylfolate supplement is a different exposure from polyglutamated 5-MTHF in a vegetable [1,2].

## Dietary Context

Two dietary routes supply this molecule.

**Direct supply.** Some foods contain preformed 5-MTHF. In the vegetables studied, much of it is polyglutamated. Those glutamate chains are shortened during intestinal absorption before the monoglutamate circulates [1].

**Endogenous conversion.** Other dietary folate forms, including folic acid after reduction by dihydrofolate reductase, enter the folate cycle and can be converted to 5-MTHF. Methylenetetrahydrofolate reductase carries out that last step [2]. A food that supplies the folate family can feed this route without being recorded as a source of preformed 5-MTHF.

### Food sources

Form-specific analysis identifies monoglutamyl and polyglutamyl 5-MTHF in raw spinach, broccoli, kale, Swiss chard and cauliflower [1]. Those five foods are the contains links below. The assay steamed the samples to inactivate plant enzymes before extraction, so the result is a raw-vegetable identification, and no amount from it is copied onto the food pages. Each of those pages also has a total-folate panel. That panel is a separate analyte.

Asparagus, lentils, chickpeas and liver are linked from their vitamin B9 records. Avocado's composition panel reports folate as the family total. Those family figures are not copied here as 5-MTHF amounts. Lentils and chickpeas are rich in total folate, and the share of each vitamer varies. Liver is very rich in total folate and contains more than 5-MTHF alone.

### Supplement versus food

L-methylfolate supplements provide isolated 5-MTHF. A vegetable portion provides the polyglutamated food form inside a whole food [1]. Folic acid in fortified foods and supplements is a different vitamer, which the body can convert toward 5-MTHF [2].
