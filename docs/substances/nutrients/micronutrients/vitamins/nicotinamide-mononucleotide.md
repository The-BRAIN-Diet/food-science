## Overview

Oxidised beta-NMN is the phosphorylated intermediate connecting NRK activity with NMN adenylyltransferase in a supported NAD-salvage route [1,2]. It is distinct from reduced NMNH.

## Dietary Context

This record resolves an endogenous conversion intermediate. It creates no oral NMN admission, food-content relationship or effective dietary dose.
