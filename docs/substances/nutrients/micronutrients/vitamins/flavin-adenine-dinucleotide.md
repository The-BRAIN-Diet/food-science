## Overview

Flavin adenine dinucleotide (FAD) is made from riboflavin through FMN. Human riboflavin kinase catalyses the first step and FLAD1 FAD synthase catalyses the second [1,2]. Glutathione reductase uses bound FAD in its NADPH-dependent regeneration of reduced glutathione [3]. These reaction identities do not establish that extra riboflavin increases recycling in every tissue.

## Dietary Context

Food riboflavin supplies an upstream precursor. A food riboflavin amount is not an amount of FAD or a measured conversion yield. No direct food-content relationship is created here.

## Research Spotlights & Evidence Checks

The established reaction route supports precursor identity. The PM6 dietary assessment additionally uses human red-cell enzyme evidence; neither source measures a food-to-FAD yield or brain recycling flux.
