## Overview

This species page provides documentation for separately registered strain identities. Taxonomic membership does not transfer intervention evidence between strains or establish dietary efficacy.

Lacticaseibacillus rhamnosus is the current species name; Lactobacillus rhamnosus is the historical name used in the original studies. [LPSN taxonomy](https://lpsn.dsmz.de/species/Lacticaseibacillus-rhamnosus).

## Lacticaseibacillus rhamnosus JB-1 {#jb-1}

The human placebo-controlled study did not reproduce overall stress/cognitive benefit. No generic probiotic, species-wide or fermented-food effect follows. [Original study](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#bravo_ingestion_2011).

Retain the human null evidence separately: [Kelly et al. (2017)](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#kelly_jb1_translation_2017).
