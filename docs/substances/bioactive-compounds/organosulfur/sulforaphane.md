## Overview

Sulforaphane is an isothiocyanate formed when glucoraphanin, present in broccoli and other cruciferous vegetables, meets the enzyme myrosinase. Chopping, crushing or chewing brings the enzyme and the precursor together. Heating can deactivate myrosinase while glucoraphanin remains. Brown mustard powder can supply active myrosinase to cooked broccoli, and gut microbes can convert some of what remains, with large differences between people [1].

It is studied because it can activate Nrf2–ARE signalling, which regulates antioxidant, detoxification and stress-response pathways [2]. Standardised broccoli-sprout preparations have shown biological effects in people. An ordinary serving of broccoli is a different exposure: the sulforaphane delivered depends on cultivar, preparation and enzyme activity [1,2]. This page does not assign one sulforaphane quantity per 100 g.

## Dietary Context

### Food sources

Broccoli and broccoli sprouts supply glucoraphanin. The foods below are linked where a sulforaphane relationship is recorded. Mature broccoli is not given a universal sulforaphane figure, because formation depends on preparation [1].

### Preparation

In one UK broccoli lot, sous-vide cooking at 100 °C for 20 minutes left 1.06 µmol sulforaphane per g dry weight. The same cooked broccoli plus 1 g brown mustard powder formed 8.58 µmol/g dry weight. That is 8.58 / 1.06 = 8.1, which the authors report as an 8-fold rise. Myrosinase activity in that cooking series fell by 40% after 2 minutes and by 90% after 6 minutes, and none was measurable after 8 minutes [1].

Twelve adults ate 200 g of the cooked broccoli. Over 24 hours they excreted 9.8 µmol sulforaphane-NAC per g creatinine, and 44.7 µmol/g creatinine when 1 g mustard powder was added. The ratio of those means is 44.7 / 9.8 = 4.6. The authors' average of each person's increase was 4.7-fold, from 1.7-fold to 10-fold. The paper does not report the glucoraphanin content of that broccoli, and it gives no moisture figure, so the dry-weight results are not converted to a fresh 100 g amount [1].

### Supplement versus food

A standardised broccoli-sprout preparation or an isolated sulforaphane dose is the exposure used in many human studies. Those results are not assumed to follow from an ordinary serving of broccoli [1,2].
