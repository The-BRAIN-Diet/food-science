## Family biology

Anthocyanins are water-soluble plant pigments (a flavonoid subclass) whose bioactive core scaffolds are the **anthocyanidins**. This canonical ontology treats the six most prevalent food anthocyanidin backbones—**Cyanidin, Delphinidin, Pelargonidin, Peonidin, Petunidin, and Malvidin**—as reusable Substance entities so Foods, Recipes, and later CUE validation can reference a stable, mechanism-linked compound layer rather than free-text pigment descriptions.
