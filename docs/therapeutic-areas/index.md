# Therapeutic Areas

Therapeutic Area pages synthesise condition-relevant evidence across the six Biological
Regulatory Systems without assuming that every mapped mechanism is abnormal in every
individual.

The reviewed evidence map currently available is:

- [ADHD](./adhd)

Therapeutic Area pages evaluate biological, dietary and lifestyle evidence. They are not claims
that a condition has one pathway, that every person shares the same biology, or that
mechanistic plausibility establishes treatment efficacy.
