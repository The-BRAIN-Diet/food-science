# The Food Composition Interpretation Register (FCIR)

## Why identity matters

Nuts and seeds are central to the BRAIN Diet. They provide fibre, protein, minerals and essential omega‑3 and omega‑6 fats the body cannot make. Composition sources often report only **18:3**—eighteen carbons and three double bonds—without saying whether that fat is **alpha-linolenic acid (ALA; 18:3 n‑3)** or **gamma-linolenic acid (GLA; 18:3 n‑6)**. This register records those identity decisions so public food and recipe values stay as precise as the evidence allows.

### What we track

We track food or material, chemical substance, and composition source as separate identities. **Substance/UNII** is the molecular identity. **Identity/source IDs** record which FDC food and nutrient fields, species, forms and papers were accepted or rejected. Mixing those layers is how an amino acid was published as an omega‑3, and how unqualified 18:3 can be treated as ALA. In eventual clinical trials, the BRAIN Diet needs to exercise precision nutrition: intake and outcome claims must rest on named compounds and documented sources, not on look-alike abbreviations or underspecified database fields.

## How to read a case

### Case ID

The **Case ID** (FCIR‑NNN) is a workflow identifier. It is not a scientific identity.

### Decision, action and source

**Decision** is what we concluded. **Action** is what the public pages and calculations now do. **Source** is the records and papers used, not a later paraphrase. The table is the canonical record.

### Missing and rejected identifiers

Unavailable food and source identifiers are **Not established** — never blank, never inferred. A rejected identifier is never treated as active provenance. GSRS/UNII is never **Not established**: use **No public GSRS match located** or **Not applicable—broad class**. GSRS searches operate on canonical substance identity, structure, CAS number and synonyms—not FDC nutrient numbers. FDC numbers belong only in the composition-source field.

### Food and recipe links

Links go to the published composition or recipe row. Food pages carry only a short pointer here.

## Register

### Identifier keys

- **FDC food ID** — identifies the composition record.
- **FDC nutrient ID** — identifies the nutrient or analytical field.
- **Scientific species name** — identifies the organism.
- **Material/form** — fruiting body, stroma, mycelium, extract, seed, oil, or other named material.
- **GSRS/UNII or another stable substance identifier** — official GSRS preferred name and UNII, linked at https://precision.fda.gov/uniisearch/srs/unii/[UNII]. Unavailable verified matches are No public GSRS match located. Broad classes are Not applicable—broad class. Not established is not used for GSRS/UNII.
- **DOI** — identifies supporting analytical literature. It is evidence provenance rather than substance identity.
