## Overview

Soy lecithin is a concentrated source of choline, supporting phosphatidylcholine synthesis and LPC-DHA transport for brain delivery.

Within the BRAIN Diet framework, precision sourcing of functional plant foods such as Mankai duckweed (iron, B12), soy/soy lecithin (choline), and fortified nutritional yeast (B12, B6) is recommended.

## Food Context

### Preparation

- Available as supplement or food additive
- Important for vegan choline intake
- Supports phospholipid-bound omega-3 strategy
- Part of functional food approach

### Essential Amino Acid Profile

Soy Lecithin provide a relatively complete plant protein (higher in lysine than most grains). Pairing with grains or other legumes still supports dietary variety and amino acid balance.
