## Overview

Pears are a whole fruit eaten fresh, poached or baked. The edible flesh provides fibre and natural sugars [1]. Bartlett pears contain pectin; the balance of pectic fractions changes as the fruit ripens, and this page does not give a per-100 g pectin amount [2].

## Food Context

### Preparation

- Eat ripe fruit fresh, or poach or bake it.
- Leave the skin on when tolerated.
