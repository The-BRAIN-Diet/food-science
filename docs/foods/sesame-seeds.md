## Overview

Sesame seeds are the whole seed of *Sesamum indicum*. Whole, unhulled seed is energy-dense, with predominantly unsaturated fat — chiefly **linoleic acid** and **oleic acid** — plus plant protein, fibre, and the characteristic lignans **sesamin** and **sesamolin**. Measured calcium is much higher in whole seed than in hulled kernels because a large share of the mineral load sits in the hull [1,2].

Within a BRAIN-aligned pattern, sesame is one plant source whether eaten as seeds or as [tahini](/docs/foods/tahini). Practical portions are small (about 15–30 g). Human feeding of 25 g sesame daily raised lignan exposure without improving blood lipids, blood pressure or inflammatory markers [3]. The protein is relatively lysine-limited, so legumes are the useful complement [4]. Sesame is a major food allergen [5].

## Other Nutritional Highlights

- The calcium value in the table is for whole, dried, unhulled seed. It should not be assumed for hulled sesame or ordinary hulled tahini [1].
- Table minerals are amounts present, not amounts absorbed. Phytate in the seed matrix reduces mineral bioavailability [6].

## Food Context

### Sourcing

- Whole or unhulled seed retains the hull and therefore more measured calcium than decorticated kernels [1].
- Raw and toasted forms differ in flavour and in lignan profile. High-heat roasting can convert sesamolin toward sesamol; that is a processing change, not evidence that roasting is universally superior [2].
- Hulled sesame is milder and lighter in colour. Do not treat it as compositionally interchangeable with whole seed.

### Synergies

- Pair with legumes to improve lysine coverage. Grains are typically lysine-limited as well and are not a lysine rescue for sesame [4].
- A vitamin-C-rich food in the same meal can support non-haem iron absorption; that is mixed-meal iron evidence, not a sesame trial [6].
- Dietary fat in the seed can help absorb carotenoids from vegetables eaten with it; the clinical demonstration used salad dressings, not sesame specifically [7].
- Sesame counts once toward weekly plant diversity whether the form is seeds or tahini. “Thirty plants per week” is a practical target from observational microbiome work, not a proven biological threshold or an RCT-derived prescription [8].

### Preparation

- Use whole, toasted or ground seed according to the dish. Grinding improves physical accessibility of nutrients but does not remove phytate or other antinutritional factors [1,6].
- Toast for flavour if wanted; avoid treating darker roast as a health upgrade [2].

### Essential Amino Acid Profile

Sesame seeds provide plant protein but are not a complete protein; lysine is typically limiting for nuts and seeds [4].

Protein pairing strategy:

Pair with legumes to improve lysine coverage. Grains are typically lysine-limited as well and should not be treated as the lysine complement to sesame.

### Safety

Sesame is a major regulated food allergen. Reactions can be severe, and cross-contamination of processed foods matters. People with a known sesame allergy should avoid seeds, tahini and sesame oil unless their clinician advises otherwise [5].

## References

[1] Elleuch et al. (2007). [Quality characteristics of sesame seeds and by-products](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#elleuch_sesame_2007). Whole seed, coat and dehulled fractions: a large share of the mineral load, including calcium, is located in the hull, so dehulling lowers measured calcium relative to whole seed.

[2] Andargie et al. (2021). [Lignans of Sesame (Sesamum indicum L.): A Comprehensive Review](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#andargie_sesame_lignans_2021). Sesamin and sesamolin are the major native lignans; content varies by cultivar, seed fraction and processing. Sesamol is a roasting and oil-processing product present only in traces in unroasted seed.

[3] Wu et al. (2009). [Sesame supplementation does not improve cardiovascular disease risk markers in overweight men and women](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#wu_sesame_2009). Randomised crossover (n = 33): 25 g/day sesame for five weeks (~50 mg/day sesame lignans) increased urinary enterolignans about eight-fold but did not change lipids, ambulatory blood pressure, CRP, IL-6, TNF-α or F2-isoprostanes.

[4] Mariotti and Gardner (2019). [Dietary Protein and Amino Acids in Vegetarian Diets—A Review](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#mariotti_dietary_2019). Nut and seed proteins are typically lysine-limited, as are cereal proteins; legumes are the lysine-rich plant complement.

[5] Adatia et al. (2017). [Sesame allergy: current perspectives](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#adatia_sesame_allergy_2017). Clinical review: sesame is a priority food allergen with mandatory labelling in the EU, Canada and Australia, and a recognised cross-contamination risk.

[6] Hallberg et al. (1989). [Iron absorption in man: ascorbic acid and dose-dependent inhibition by phytate](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#hallberg_iron_1989). Human iron-absorption series: phytate inhibits non-haem iron absorption in a dose-dependent way; ascorbic acid can counteract that inhibition. Mixed-meal evidence, not a sesame trial.

[7] Brown et al. (2004). [Carotenoid bioavailability is higher from salads ingested with full-fat than with fat-reduced salad dressings](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#brown_carotenoid_2004). Human salad study of co-ingested dietary fat and carotenoid absorption. General lipid-phase mechanism; not a sesame-seed trial.

[8] McDonald et al. (2018). [American Gut: an Open Platform for Citizen Science Microbiome Research](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#mcdonald_american_gut_2018). Observational citizen-science cohort: self-reported intake of more than 30 plant types per week, versus 10 or fewer, associated with greater representation of putative fermenters. Not a randomised trial and not a proven threshold.

[9] Muñoz-Esparza et al. (2021). [Occurrence of Polyamines in Foods and the Influence of Cooking Processes](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#munoz_esparza_food_polyamines_2021). Analytical evidence for spermidine occurrence; food type and preparation affect composition.
