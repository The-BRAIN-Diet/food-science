## Overview

Brazil nuts are the seeds of *Bertholletia excelsa*. USDA SR Legacy records **1917 µg selenium per 100 g** for dried, unblanched nuts, and **543 µg in the 1 oz (28.35 g) portion** of that same record [1].

That ounce is a large selenium amount. The selenium guidance is to avoid frequent high-dose intake, because selenium has a narrower safety margin than many other minerals.

## Other Nutritional Highlights

- Selenium 1917 µg per 100 g, and 543 µg per 1 oz (28.35 g), on the dried unblanched USDA record [1].
- The same record also reports magnesium 376 mg, phosphorus 725 mg and copper 1.743 mg per 100 g [1].
- A usual portion is a small number of nuts. The per-100 g figure is not a serving.

## Food Context

### Preparation

- Use dried nuts in small portions.
- This page records the dried, unblanched USDA food. It is not a butter, flour or extract.
