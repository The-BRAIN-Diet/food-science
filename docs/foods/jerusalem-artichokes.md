## Overview

Jerusalem artichokes contain **fructans** at 8.99 ± 0.238 g per 100 g fresh weight. That figure is reported separately from fructooligosaccharides and is not a combined fructo-oligosaccharide and galacto-oligosaccharide amount [3].

The fructan figure is the food record. It is not an oligofructose amount, and it is not a butyrate yield from eating the tuber [3].

## Food Context

### Preparation

- Can be consumed raw or cooked
- Fructans were 8.99 ± 0.238 g per 100 g fresh weight, reported separately from fructooligosaccharides [3]
- Start with small amounts to avoid digestive discomfort
- Supports gut microbiome diversity
