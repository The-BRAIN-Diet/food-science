## Overview

Duck fat is a culinary fat used for roasting vegetables and pan cooking. The nutrient table is rendered duck fat.

## Key Nutritional Highlights

- Rendered duck fat supplies 99.8 g fat per 100 g, including 33.2 g saturated fat.
- Vitamin D is 4.8 µg per 100 g.

## Food Context

### Synergies

- Part of diverse fat strategy

### Preparation

- Suitable for roasting vegetables and pan cooking
- Supports MUFA intake
- Heat-stable cooking fat
