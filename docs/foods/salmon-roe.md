## Overview

Salmon roe provides **DHA** and **EPA** within a phospholipid-rich matrix [1,2], alongside **choline**, **vitamin B12**, **astaxanthin** and high-quality protein. This unusually concentrated combination makes small portions useful in preparations such as Neuroeshot. The nutrient table is salted chum salmon ikura (*Oncorhynchus keta*).

## Key Nutritional Highlights

- Roe is a phospholipid-rich marine matrix. The cited mechanistic studies concern phospholipid-form DHA, but they do not establish the proportion of EPA or DHA in particular lipid classes for this preparation [1,2].
- **Astaxanthin** (carotenoid) is present, but its amount in a particular commercial product is not established without product-relevant analysis.
- The table is cured ikura. Sodium in that record is part of the salt cure.
- **Freshness and cold chain** matter: oxidation risk rises with poor storage even if the matrix is phospholipid-bound.

## Food Context

### Synergies

- Pair with vegetables and whole grains as a flavour-dense, small-portion ingredient rather than a bulk protein source.
- Vitamin C–rich foods support iron balance in mixed meals (roe provides modest iron).

### Sourcing

- Prefer reputable suppliers with clear species and harvest region; frozen ikura is common and acceptable when thawed safely.
- Colour and texture vary by species, diet, and season — not a reliable purity test on its own.
- The composition table is salted chum salmon (*Oncorhynchus keta*) ikura.

### Preparation

- Traditionally eaten **cold** or lightly warmed (on rice, in sushi, or as a spoonful) to limit heat damage to unsaturated fats.
- Avoid prolonged high heat; treat as a delicate fat + protein ingredient.
- If the roe tastes heavily cured, a brief rinse in clean cold water followed by thorough draining can remove surface brine and reduce saltiness. A very short cold-water soak may reduce it further, but the resulting sodium content cannot be calculated from the original label. Keep the roe cold and avoid prolonged soaking, which can impair texture [3].

### Essential Amino Acid Profile

This food provides a complete essential amino acid profile typical of animal proteins.

## References

[1] Liu et al. (2014). [Higher efficacy of dietary DHA provided as a phospholipid than as a triglyceride for brain DHA accretion in neonatal piglets](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#liu_higher_2014). Neonatal piglets given a single oral dose of 13C-DHA as phosphatidylcholine accumulated about 1.9-fold more labeled DHA in brain gray matter than piglets given the same DHA as triglyceride.

[2] Patrick (2019). [Role of phosphatidylcholine-DHA in preventing APOE4-associated Alzheimer's disease](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#patrick_role_2019). Review: phosphatidylcholine-DHA can be converted to lysophosphatidylcholine-DHA, which uses MFSD2A transport into the brain more efficiently than triglyceride-bound or unesterified DHA. Mechanistic context for roe's phospholipid DHA, not a salmon-roe feeding study.
