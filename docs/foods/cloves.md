## Overview

Cloves are the dried flower buds of *Syzygium aromaticum*, used as a spice in small amounts. Chromatography without hydrolysis reports one **quercetin** determination in a commercial dried bud: 28.4 mg per 100 g fresh weight [1] [2]. That figure is a single sample. It is not a range, and it is not a result from chromatography after hydrolysis.

Within the BRAIN Diet framework, cloves remain a seasoning. A per-100 g value is not a serving estimate.

## Key Nutritional Highlights

- Quercetin 28.4 mg per 100 g fresh weight in dried clove bud (one determination; chromatography without hydrolysis) [1] [2].
- USDA ground cloves supply about 274 kcal per 100 g. Culinary use is a gram-scale portion.
- The same USDA panel is high in manganese, about 60 mg per 100 g. That concentration is not a reason to eat cloves in quantity.

## Food Context

### Preparation

- Use whole or ground cloves in culinary amounts.
- The quercetin value is for dried bud, not an infusion or an extract.
