## Overview

The nutrient table is Marigold Engevita nutritional yeast flakes with added B12. This dried inactive yeast is fortified with B vitamins and zinc.

Within the BRAIN Diet framework, fortified nutritional yeast (B12, B6) helps narrow vegan nutrient gaps, and precision sourcing of functional plant foods such as Mankai duckweed (iron, B12), soy/soy lecithin (choline), and fortified nutritional yeast (B12, B6) is recommended.

## Key Nutritional Highlights

- The nutrient table is Marigold Engevita flakes with added B12. The label serving is 5 g; the table is per 100 g of the dry product.

### Synergies

- Can be used as seasoning or supplement
- Important for vegan/vegetarian diets

### Essential Amino Acid Profile

Nutritional Yeast contribute plant protein. Pair with complementary protein sources (e.g. grains and legumes) for a balanced essential amino acid profile.
