## Overview

Avocado oil is a monounsaturated-fat culinary oil whose main fatty acid is **oleic acid** [1]. Composition varies with cultivar and processing, so it should not be treated as one fixed oil.

Within the BRAIN Diet, it can help absorb carotenoids from salads and salsa [2]. Prefer whole avocado when fibre and potassium also matter. Fat quality, oil grade and the rest of the meal still matter more than isolated oil intake.

## Key Nutritional Highlights

- **Oleic acid** is the main fatty acid; composition varies by cultivar and processing [1].
- Adding avocado oil to salad helps absorb carotenoids from vegetables [2].
- Refined and extra-virgin grades differ; prefer whole avocado when fibre and potassium also matter [1].
- Use as a cooking or dressing fat in moderation.

## Food Context

### Synergies

- Pair with carotenoid-rich vegetables; avocado oil and avocado fruit both enhanced carotenoid absorption in the same human trial [2].

### Preparation

- Smoke point and heat stability depend on refining and product quality; extra-virgin avocado oil should not be assumed to have a higher smoke point than extra-virgin olive oil [1,3].

## References

[1] Fernandes et al. (2018). [Chemical characterization of commercial and single-variety avocado oils](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#fernandes_avocado_oil_2018). Specialist characterisation of commercial and single-variety avocado oils; Woolf and colleagues proposed oleic at 60–80% of fatty acids, with cultivar and processing changing the profile, and the 67.889 g/100 g figure on this page is from USDA SR Legacy Oil, avocado, not from this paper.

[2] Unlu et al. (2005). [Carotenoid Absorption from Salad and Salsa by Humans Is Enhanced by the Addition of Avocado or Avocado Oil](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#unlu_carotenoid_2005). Avocado oil (and avocado fruit) added to salad increased human absorption of α-carotene, β-carotene and lutein versus salad without avocado lipid.

[3] Flores et al. (2019). [Avocado Oil: Characteristics, Properties, and Applications](/docs/papers/BRAIN-Diet-References#flores_avocado_oil_2019). Review of avocado-oil composition and applications; thermal stability is similar to olive oil, so smoke point is not treated as universally higher.
