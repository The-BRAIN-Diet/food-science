## Overview

Extra virgin olive oil is a culinary oil rich in oleic acid. It contains phenolics including oleocanthal, oleacein, hydroxytyrosol and tyrosol. Amounts vary with the olives, harvest, processing and storage. Oleuropein-related derivatives in oil should be distinguished from intact oleuropein.

## Food Context

### Preparation

- Use for dressings, drizzling and cooking.
- Store away from heat and light.
- Harvest timing alone does not guarantee a particular phenolic or CoQ10 concentration.
