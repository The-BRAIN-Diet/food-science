# Annex D — Primary-mechanism research completeness

Flags describe the state of the records. They are not a Scientific Evidence Confidence score. A missing score, including `not-yet-scored`, is not the reason for a flag.

- **Ready for V1 dietary extraction** — at least one `dietary_input: true` relationship is scientifically admitted and has a verified quantitative food observation.
- **Food composition/ontology incomplete** — an admitted input has no food target, an unresolved identity, or a scientific admission other than admitted.
- **SOP research incomplete** — no SOP atom is recorded, or the structured SOP and candidate text does not cover preparation, food-matrix context, meal timing or fermentation.
- **Lifestyle research incomplete** — no lifestyle-priority atom is recorded.
- **Mechanistic/Scientific Finding reassessment needed** — a dietary-input relationship is not scientifically admitted.
- **No currently admitted dietary contribution** — the mechanism has no `dietary_input: true` relationship.

A mechanism may carry more than one flag. Readiness for food extraction does not close the SOP or lifestyle question.
