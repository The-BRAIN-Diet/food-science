BRS3-2-3 - Nrf2-Mediated Cellular Defence Regulation

(Regulating cellular defence gene expression)

1. Mission & Overview + Dietary Levers

Mission

Regulate the expression of cellular defence genes in response to oxidative and electrophilic stress.

Intervention Dominance: Diet-Supported — Dietary Requirements

The homogenate changed nasal enzyme RNA. It is not ordinary broccoli, not a capsule, and not brain Nrf2.

  • Sulforaphane

No evidence-supported cofactors or substrates are currently established for this mechanism.

No mapping established.

Overview

Nrf2 is held by Keap1 until oxidative or electrophilic stress lets it reach defence genes. Itoh et al. (1999) showed that Keap1 represses Nrf2 and that electrophiles release that repression. [7] Zhang et al. (2004) showed that Keap1 marks Nrf2 for disposal until stress or sulforaphane slows that disposal. [8] A three-day broccoli sprout homogenate raised phase II enzyme RNA in nasal cells. [5] That is airway gene expression, not brain Nrf2, and sulforaphane is not a nutrient this regulation requires.

  • Benefits: This regulation lets cells raise defence-gene transcription when oxidative or electrophilic stress rises. [7]
  • Implementation Notes: The tested practice is a myrosinase-completed broccoli sprout homogenate once a day for three days. [5] Capsules of extracted sulforaphane did not raise the same class of genes in COPD lung cells. [6]
  • Biological Relevance: The enzymes this regulation induces are used by later clearance, membrane, and recycling steps — see PM4, PM5 and PM6.

2. Primary Biological Effects

Defence-gene transcription rises when stress releases Nrf2 from Keap1

4. Mechanistic Basis

Summary

Cellular defence-gene expression is regulated when oxidative or electrophilic stress releases Nrf2 from Keap1. That regulation sits within BRS3(FM2) - Antioxidant Defense Capacity.

(Nrf2-ARE signalling)

Itoh et al. (1999) showed that Keap1 represses Nrf2 until electrophiles release that hold. [7] Zhang et al. (2004) showed that Keap1 marks Nrf2 for disposal, and that oxidative stress or sulforaphane slows that disposal. [8] Raising the response is not, by itself, the aim of this regulation.

(Isothiocyanate signalling and induction kinetics)

Riedl, Saxon and Diaz-Sanchez (2009) found that a three-day broccoli sprout homogenate, finished with daikon myrosinase, raised phase II enzyme RNA in nasal cells. [5] Wise et al. (2016) found that four weeks of extracted sulforaphane capsules did not raise Nrf2 target genes in COPD lung cells. [6] Sulforaphane is an inducer in this preparation, not a nutrient the regulation requires.

(Boundaries of the mechanism)

Net ROS generation versus clearance dynamics are handled by BRS3-FM2-PM4 - ROS Generation vs Clearance Balance. Membrane lipid protection belongs to BRS3-FM2-PM5 - Lipid Peroxidation Control. Antioxidant-network recycling and regeneration are represented by BRS3-FM2-PM6 - Antioxidant Network Recycling.

(Integration within BRS3)

This PM supports endogenous defence capacity within BRS3(FM2). Vitamin C, zinc and selenium are not established here as requirements of the activation step.

4.1 Scientific Findings (2)

Summary

Keap1 holds Nrf2 until oxidative or electrophilic stress lets defence genes be transcribed. A three-day myrosinase-completed broccoli sprout homogenate raised phase II enzyme RNA in nasal cells. Extracted sulforaphane capsules did not raise that class of genes in COPD lung cells. Sulforaphane is not a nutrient this regulation requires.

Keap1 binds Nrf2 and keeps defence-gene transcription off. Electrophiles and oxidative stress release that hold. Keap1 also sends Nrf2 for disposal, and sulforaphane or oxidative stress slows that disposal. Turning the response up is not, by itself, the aim.

What this means

This is the control step. It is not a nutrient requirement, and it is not evidence that more activity is always useful.

Evidence confidence: Not yet scored

Finding ID: PM3-F1

Finding Statement: Keap1 represses Nrf2 until oxidative or electrophilic stress allows defence-gene transcription. Sulforaphane can slow Keap1-directed disposal of Nrf2 in cells. That is regulation, not a dietary requirement.

Synthesised Evidence Confidence: Not yet scored

Synthesis: Itoh and colleagues identified Keap1 as the protein that binds Nrf2 and represses antioxidant-response-element activity. Electrophilic agents opposed that repression and let Nrf2 move toward the nucleus. Zhang and colleagues showed Keap1 is the adaptor that marks Nrf2 for disposal, and that oxidative stress or sulforaphane inhibits that marking. Neither paper is a dietary trial.

Synthesis Limitations: Both results are cell biology. They do not measure brain Nrf2, and they do not show that eating more of any nutrient improves this regulation.

Evidence Considered:

  • Study
    Identification of Keap1 as the repressor of Nrf2, with tests of electrophilic agents in cells.
    Population
    Transfected cell lines. No human cohort.
    Result
    Keap1 holds Nrf2 back, and electrophiles release that hold so defence genes can be transcribed.
    Effect / Magnitude
    The paper reports repression and its release. It does not give a human fold change.
    Evidence Summary
    Defence-gene transcription is a regulated response to oxidative and electrophilic stress.
    Limitations
    Cell identification of the sensor is not a food result and not a brain measurement.
    Evidence Source
    Bounded external search

  • Study
    Cell and biochemical tests of Keap1 as the adaptor that marks Nrf2 for disposal.
    Population
    Cultured cells. No human dietary cohort.
    Result
    Stress or sulforaphane slows disposal of Nrf2. Changing cysteine 151 of Keap1 blocks that effect.
    Effect / Magnitude
    The paper reports inhibition of ubiquitination, not a dietary dose.
    Evidence Summary
    Sulforaphane acts on the Keap1 sensor in cells. It is not a nutrient the regulation requires.
    Limitations

Supports SOP: Myrosinase-completed broccoli sprout homogenate

Healthy nonsmokers drank a broccoli sprout homogenate, finished with daikon myrosinase, once a day for three days. Phase II enzyme RNA rose in nasal cells. Alfalfa did not do this. Capsules of extracted sulforaphane for four weeks did not raise Nrf2 target genes in COPD lung cells.

What this means

The homogenate is a preparation that changed an airway readout of this gene programme. It is not a nutrient requirement, not brain Nrf2, and not evidence that a capsule does the same thing.

Evidence confidence: Not yet scored

Finding ID: PM3-F2

Finding Statement: A myrosinase-completed broccoli sprout homogenate raised phase II enzyme RNA in nasal cells of healthy adults. Extracted sulforaphane capsules did not raise Nrf2 target-gene expression in COPD lung cells.

Synthesised Evidence Confidence: Not yet scored

Synthesis: Riedl, Saxon and Diaz-Sanchez gave healthy nonsmokers a broccoli sprout homogenate blended with daikon sprouts and held at 37°C, once a day for three days. Nasal phase II enzyme RNA rose with dose. Alfalfa sprout homogenate did not. Wise and colleagues gave people with COPD 25 or 150 micromoles of extracted sulforaphane, or placebo, for four weeks. Nrf2 target-gene expression in alveolar macrophages and bronchial epithelium did not rise, and inflammation and lung function did not change.

Synthesis Limitations: Nasal RNA after three days is not brain Nrf2. The capsule null is a different preparation in COPD, so it does not cancel the homogenate result, and the homogenate result does not make capsules work.

Evidence Considered:

  • Study
    Placebo-style food comparison in healthy nonsmokers. Broccoli sprout homogenate versus alfalfa sprout homogenate, once daily for three days.
    Population
    Healthy nonsmoking adults. Nasal cells sampled after the third dose.
    Result
    Phase II enzyme RNA in nasal cells rose with the broccoli sprout dose and did not rise with alfalfa.
    Effect / Magnitude
    The rise was dose-related. Nrf2 protein was not measured, so no Nrf2 fold change is available.
    Evidence Summary
    A defined sprout preparation can raise airway defence-gene RNA.
    Limitations
    Airway RNA is not brain Nrf2, not an ordinary broccoli serving, and not a clinical outcome.
    Evidence Source
    Inherited repository evidence
    Reference
    [5]
  • Study
    Randomised comparison of two sulforaphane capsule doses with placebo for four weeks.
    Population
    People with COPD.
    Result
    Nrf2 target-gene expression in alveolar macrophages and bronchial epithelium did not rise. Inflammation markers and lung function did not differ.
    Effect / Magnitude
    No rise in the measured target genes at either 25 or 150 micromoles a day.
    Evidence Summary
    Extracted sulforaphane capsules did not reproduce the homogenate gene result.
    Limitations
    COPD lung cells are not healthy nasal cells, and capsules are not the myrosinase-completed homogenate.
    Evidence Source
    Inherited repository evidence
    Reference

5.3 Local BRS Mechanism Relationships

Related Primary Mechanisms within the same Biological Regulatory System that collectively support the integrated biological function.

  • BRS3-FM2-PM4 - ROS Generation vs Clearance Balance — Clearance enzymes set net oxidative pressure. This page regulates expression of those defence genes. It is not the clearance balance.
  • BRS3-FM2-PM5 - Lipid Peroxidation Control — Lipid peroxidation is membrane damage downstream of oxidative load. This regulation does not itself stop that chain reaction.
  • BRS3-FM2-PM6 - Antioxidant Network Recycling — Recycling regenerates vitamin and thiol antioxidants after use. This page regulates defence-gene expression and does not restore the spent vitamins.
