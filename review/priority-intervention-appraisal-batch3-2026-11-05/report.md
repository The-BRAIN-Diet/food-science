## Exact proposed first lines

- **BRS4-FM1-PM1**: Eight-week endurance cycling → within-group increase in muscle mitochondrial respiration
- **BRS-X(ECS-PM2)**: Tested low-LA/high-EPA/DHA diet → higher circulating DHA-ethanolamide
- **BRS3-FM2-PM5**: Tested phenolic-rich olive oil → lower circulating oxidised LDL
- **BRS6-FM3-PM6**: Six-minute guided breathing after a speech stressor → higher recovery RMSSD
- **BRS-X(Circadian-FM1-PM1)**: Tested evening blue-blocking glasses → earlier salivary melatonin onset
- **BRS4-FM4-PM9**: Six-month calorie restriction → higher muscle mitochondrial-DNA content

## Decisions requiring attention

- Urolithin A stops at eligibility: PM5 explicitly excludes mitophagy/removal. No new PM or mission change is proposed. Historical PM9 discussion does not override its biogenesis mission.
- Endurance cycling is mechanism-relevant, but the paper’s explicit control superiority concerns combined training. Do not import that contrast; defer admission of a causal endurance-only claim.
- The other five recommendations are bounded to measured endpoints and tested exposure/population. Numerical SEC does not itself approve admission.
- Olive-oil evidence belongs to lipid-oxidation PM5, not NF-κB activation. The existing olive-oil food page was inspected: composition and cooking evidence are not substitutes for EUROLIVE.
- Preliminary discovery corrections: no verified six-breaths/minute prescription; HF did not significantly differ from passive control; 62 feeding-trial participants reached four weeks, not all twelve weeks; plasma DHA-ethanolamide is not production flux.
- No new collapsed Mechanism summaries are proposed: full MoA qualifications remain inside Atom 3. Missing controlled endpoint/preparation identities must not be invented during later integration.
