## Recommendations and boundaries

**Propose two bounded admissions, not general dietary prescriptions:** glutamine only for the tested postinfectious IBS-D/hyperpermeability population and protocol; egg-yolk phospholipid only as the tested acute choline-provision comparison. Both remain Unset review proposals until author approval. The latter is a 37 g isolated phospholipid preparation supplying 3 g choline, not ordinary eggs and not a routine daily dose recommendation.

The existing contract explicitly permits an SOP acting through a connected mechanism when its measured effect and PM relevance remain separate (`system/dietary-input-traceability-contract.md`, “PM-owned SOP and lifestyle relationships”). Therefore absent brain measurements do not automatically exclude an upstream provision practice. PM6-F4 and PM6-DIT-2 already establish the phosphatidylcholine-to-circulating-choline route. The proposed egg record does not claim increased acetylcholine, and is not duplicated onto BRS2-FM3-PM7 as evidence of Kennedy/PEMT synthesis.

**Deferrals:** whey has a relevant source-appearance result, but the pooled comparison is not a reproducible matched protocol; extract the relevant constituent trial before recommending a source switch. Vitamin C/E needs original-methods and kinetic-uncertainty appraisal. The prebiotic blend needs a recoverable exact material specification; its secondary IPA signal is marginal and unadjusted. Fish/capsules needs a distinct intervention purpose beyond existing DHA provision: no superiority was demonstrated and a nonsignificant contrast cannot establish equivalence. These are not blanket requirements for downstream clinical efficacy.

**Reject the cocoa assignment to PM12 as currently proposed:** the human trial measured cerebral blood volume and task performance, not physical synaptic remodelling. Separate mouse spine evidence cited by the authors cannot be silently substituted for the human intervention. No alternate PM was created or mission broadened.

## Source and appraisal notes

Current local discovery records and canonical admissions were used, including uncommitted work. PM1’s saved Gorissen appraisal was reused and cross-checked against the primary text; its existing Finding score was not inherited. None of the seven exact protocols has an active intervention-specific SEC pin in its proposed PM. Glutamine already has a dietary admission and a Finding; choline/phosphatidylcholine and DHA are also existing dietary inputs, not new discoveries.

1. **Gorissen (2020):** pooled 18 trials from one programme; 602 participants, predominantly men. Five-hour fractions differ (whey 57±10%, casein 45±11%, milk 65±13%); milk outperformed whey for that integrated endpoint. Age, dose and protocol context differ. Independent replication of a matched whey/casein intervention is not inferred from the number of contributing trials.

2. **Bruno (2006):** primary abstract retrieved; Scite supplied no readable body and publisher access returned 403. Smokers showed slower labelled tocopherol disappearance; nonsmokers did not. F2-isoprostanes did not improve. B/P remain pending with two separately actionable Assessment gaps in `appraisals.json`. A linked 2007 surrogate-endpoint critique and author reply require review; neither is treated as a retraction.

3. **Kang (2022):** full primary body inspected. Twenty completers from 24 randomised; four-week crossover arms and washout. IPA P=0.04 is an unadjusted secondary signal, not the primary bifidobacterial endpoint. No detected changes in TMAO, faecal SCFAs, diversity or cardiometabolic profiles. A 12 g packet is not necessarily 12 g pure fibre. Ingredient identities were recovered, proportions were not. PM8’s current Findings explicitly include microbial tryptophan/indole processing, so eligibility does not require broadening its mission.

4. **Zhou (online 2018; issue 2019):** full primary body and tables verified. Glutamine 5 g three times daily for eight weeks versus whey-protein control. The earlier discovery shorthand “0.05 versus 0.11” mixed treatment-end with baseline: Table 2 gives end values 0.05 versus 0.10; Table 3 gives changes −0.06±0.03 versus −0.0004±0.03, P<0.0001. Precision 2 concerns that narrow directional permeability proposition, not clinical importance or broad efficacy. Completer analysis and inconsistent withdrawal prose retain Bias 1.

5. **Smolders (2019):** full primary body inspected. Randomised blinded acute crossover in 18 adults; plasma choline iAUC was about fourfold higher with the phospholipid drink. Formulation, high dose and six-hour window are inseparable from the proposal. No plasma phospholipid-DHA response and no TAG-DHA difference after dose correction. Do not describe the plasma response as a measured fraction absorbed or infer central choline delivery.

6. **Brickman (2014):** primary Introduction and Results checked for eligibility; no full SEC appraisal. Three-month high/low-flavanol comparison; DG blood volume and a specific reaction-time task changed. Delayed retention and exercise-related fitness outcomes did not. The structural bridge rests on separate animal evidence.

7. **Harris (2007):** primary abstract and source identity checked; full appraisal stopped at uncertain eligibility. The headline red-cell endpoint is **EPA+DHA**, not DHA alone. Both groups reached approximately 6.2%; there was no significant overall group effect. This cannot inherit the existing older-adult CNS protocol’s evidence.

All numerical scores are provisional review judgements under the existing D/B/P/R convention, not scientific approval. R=0 means no independently appraised exact confirmation is credited; it does not assert that related literature does not exist. Calibration proposal text has not been promoted into new policy. SEC does not determine admission automatically.

Scite metadata did not supply an editorialNotices field. A retraction-filter query returned no matches; this is not comprehensive clearance. Primary source-access limits are preserved. Scientific uncertainty and incomplete appraisal are separate; no canonical Evidence Gap or Assessment gap record was created.
