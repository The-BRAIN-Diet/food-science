## Required qualifications retained inside the six atoms

**Wastyk:** four-week ramp plus six-week maintenance, 18 participants per arm, healthy adults. The primary cytokine response score was null in both arms. The 19/93 serum-protein changes were exploratory; microbial diversity increased as a separate endpoint and does not establish mediation or clinical benefit. IL-10 is not simply a harmful inflammatory marker. No usual-diet control; no independent claim for an individual fermented food. Do not automatically assign the pattern to BRS5 ecological selection.

**Baxter:** extracted unmodified potato starch, predominantly type-2 resistant starch, administered unheated; 40–48 g supplement/day, approximately 70% RS2 (roughly 28–34 g resistant fraction), with a 4–7-day dose ramp before full-dose sampling. This is starch supplement, not whole potato flour, cooked/cooled potatoes or a banana meal. Potato arm n=43. Average faecal butyrate concentration rose about 29%; 37% had unchanged/decreased concentration. Concentration is not production flux or barrier/brain exposure. Maize RS and inulin did not significantly raise butyrate. The earlier appraisal's shorthand “potato flour” must not be interpreted as whole-potato flour in an implementation.

**Sutton:** six-hour daily window, dinner completed by 15:00, five weeks, matched weight-maintenance food versus twelve-hour window; eight male completers with prediabetes. Endpoint is the OGTT incremental insulin/glucose AUC ratio, not clamp-measured disposal. Fasting/OGTT glucose and inflammatory markers were unchanged; fasting lipid increases and unequal pretest fasting remain limitations. No generic fasting or circadian-mechanism claim.
