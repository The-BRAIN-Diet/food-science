# BRS1-FM1-PM4

Mission: Support noradrenergic alertness and executive control so attention and arousal stay appropriately engaged.

Existing: no structured SOP/Lifestyle admissions. Removed reader-facing template instruction from the SOP empty state; replaced the Lifestyle statement about exercise not being a dietary requirement with an intervention-specific evidence boundary. Mission, Findings and dietary records unchanged.

Research: [McGowan 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30995458/) used a counterbalanced treadmill/control crossover, 20 minutes at approximately 70% predicted maximum heart rate; pupillometric LC indices were not modulated. [Kuwamizu 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37323937/) and [pupil/prefrontal study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37353097/) support an indirect arousal/LC hypothesis under very light exercise. Different protocols and proxy specificity preclude a generalized noradrenaline claim; candidate retained for research, not admitted.

[Cold/hypoxia tyrosine experiment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2736402/) used 100 mg/kg and 4.5-hour exposure; cognitive results do not isolate central noradrenaline. [Older-adult cooling study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28477375/) measured cutaneous sympathetic vasoconstriction, outside this PM's central attention mission. No supplement or cold-exposure recommendation admitted. Animal cold-stress neurochemistry is not silently converted to a human dietary dose. Sleep/stress relevance remains background without a verified reproducible protocol in this pass.

Dominance: no newly established principal SOP/Lifestyle route; existing qualification preserved. No category filled artificially. Paper use: proxy-mechanism caution only, not a new recommended intervention. Retrieval used primary abstracts and indexed publisher descriptions; full protocol adjudication of the positive pupil studies remains a limitation.
