[Kuwamizu 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37323937/) and [pupil/prefrontal study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37353097/) support an indirect arousal/LC hypothesis under very light exercise. No supplement or cold-exposure recommendation admitted. Animal cold-stress neurochemistry is not silently converted to a human dietary dose. **Lifestyle evidence:** Yamazaki et al. (2023), *A possible contribution of the locus coeruleus to arousal enhancement with mild exercise*, [DOI 10.1093/texcom/tgad010](https://doi.org/10.1093/texcom/tgad010), full text read through Scite. Twenty-one young men completed random-order exercise/rest sessions: ten minutes cycling at 30% VO2peak, 60 rpm. Pupil/arousal changes correlated with structural LC contrast measured on another day; baseline correlations were null.
