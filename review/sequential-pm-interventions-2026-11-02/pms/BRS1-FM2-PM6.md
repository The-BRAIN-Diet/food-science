External follow-up after this screen recovered original choline-form comparisons through Scite. Smolders et al. (2019), DOI https://doi.org/10.3390/nu11112758: 18 healthy adults, randomised double-blind crossover, 37 g freeze-dried egg phospholipids delivering 3 g choline versus matched choline-bitartrate shake; higher six-hour plasma choline iAUC. This was not an ordinary egg preparation experiment. The plasma DHA contrast disappeared after dose adjustment. Böckmann et al. (2022), DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/s00394-021-02637-6: six men, randomised crossover, 550 mg choline-equivalent preparations with a butter pretzel; no significant choline AUC difference, different kinetics/TMAO. Power was based on supplementation versus baseline, not formulation equivalence, and comparisons were exploratory. Methods and results read in both sources; no equivalence claim follows from the second trial's null.
