## Remaining candidate decisions

- Roasted nori: recovered from the existing food-page reference; [Huang 2024 full methods/results/discussion](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00394-024-03505-9) inspected. Thirty vegetarians, open-label randomised 4-week comparison, 5/8 g daily versus none. Trial batch supplied approximately 1.9/3.1 µg B12, not the planned 2.4/4. Adjusted B12/holoTC/homocysteine responses support upstream supply; between-group MMA was nonsignificant. This is not tissue flux or a replacement for deficiency treatment. Pending SOP classification and product-context reconciliation; not admitted or rejected from lack of brain measurements.
- Stam 2005 (10.1042/CS20040295): existing Finding and primary abstract support isotope flux at pharmacological 5 mg folic acid/day. Full methods needed before adding a conditional supplement protocol.
- Exercise: PMID15860911 abstract associates exercise plus folate/B12 intake with lower circulating homocysteine. Does not distinguish remethylation from other clearance pathways; no lifestyle admission from this abstract.
