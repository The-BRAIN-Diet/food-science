## Retrieval follow-up — 2 November 2026

The earlier abstract retrieval failure is resolved. PubMed (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16218925/) and the publisher abstract (https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1365-201X.2005.01482.x) were accessible. The publisher full-text endpoint returned an access error; searches for an accessible original manuscript recovered bibliographic/abstract records, not full methods. No claim of full-text appraisal.

Blomstrand et al. 2005: five men completed two 3-hour cycling conditions at 200 ± 7 W, receiving 6% carbohydrate or flavoured-water placebo. Radial arterial/internal-jugular sampling and plasma flow estimated net cerebral amino-acid exchange. Placebo showed approximately 106 μmol tryptophan uptake; carbohydrate showed no statistically significant uptake. This within-condition significance pattern alone does not establish a significant between-condition uptake difference. BCAA uptake occurred in both conditions.

Interpretation: relevant human cerebral-exchange evidence, stronger endpoint specificity than a plasma ratio alone. Net exchange does not isolate LAT1 transport kinetics or demonstrate serotonin synthesis, benefit, or a general meal strategy. Reduced uptake is not automatically improvement. Exact drink volume/timing/composition, allocation order, flow methodology and between-condition uptake statistics remain unverified from full methods. Candidate remains deferred for protocol extraction and benefit/mission adjudication; no canonical admission or SEC change. Origin: existing external-literature lead; this targeted retrieval is not a new food-page-led discovery. Mandatory food-page screening remains pending before further discovery.
