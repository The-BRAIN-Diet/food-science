### Yoga Nidra: full primary paper recovered
Kjaer et al. 2002, DOI 10.1016/S0926-6410(01)00106-9. Full five-page primary article: https://www.yoganidranetwork.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Meditation-Dopamine.pdf . Methods p256, results/table p257, limitations p258–259 inspected.

Eight healthy male teachers (31–50; 7–26 years’ daily experience) underwent random-order PET sessions on separate days. Guided audio lasted 72 minutes; control used same-voice speech. Ventral-striatal raclopride binding fell 7.9% (reported p<0.013). The frequently quoted 65% dopamine increase was extrapolated from monkey calibration, not directly measured in humans. EEG succeeded in five. Reference-region blood-flow sensitivity and the expert-only sample limit inference. No clinical benefit was tested.

Disposition: mission-relevant acute mechanistic candidate, originating in external literature. Retrieval blocker resolved. Do not present generic meditation as a proven dopamine optimisation. A reproducible transferable protocol, generalisability and canonical evidence linkage still require adjudication before admission. No new Finding, score or intervention inserted here. A distinct new Finding is not automatically required merely because a new paper exists; determine appropriate existing linkage under the contract, without attaching it to an unrelated proposition.
