# Mandatory food-evidence reuse checkpoint

Applies to every PM in the existing sequential intervention queue. This is a task execution requirement, not a change to scientific admission standards.

1. Resolve the PM’s canonical dietary inputs and food relationships using the existing mapper. Record the actual linked substance, material, preparation and source. Do not infer PM relevance from other nutrients on the same food page.
2. Read linked food pages, including Preparation, Synergies, Bioavailability and scientific references. Record pages with no relevant intervention evidence as screened, not as candidate admissions.
3. Extract candidate practices with food state, exact preparation, population, measured endpoint and original citation. Preserve unknowns explicitly.
4. Follow original references and assess the candidate against the PM mission, SOP eligibility and existing dominance rules. A food page is an evidence-discovery source, not independent scientific evidence.
5. Only then perform additional external discovery or targeted retrieval. Existing external research is retained; it does not need to be repeated merely because this checkpoint was added later.
6. Record candidate origin, existing food-page evidence, source verification, admission disposition and potential food-page updates. Do not automatically update food pages or promote advice into PM SOPs.

## Candidate provenance fields

Candidate ID; PM ID; initial discovery origin (existing BRAIN food page / new literature research); food-page path and section; linked canonical input; food material/state; preparation or exposure; population; measured endpoint; original citation; underlying-source verification; PM relevance; disposition and reason; food-page update opportunity.

For retrospectively found overlap, distinguish the initial external discovery from the subsequent discovery that the food page already held the evidence. Do not count this as a prospective food-page-led discovery.

## Completion and reporting

A PM remains incomplete while its food-evidence checkpoint is pending. Inventory extraction alone does not constitute scientific appraisal. Prior scientific work, admissions and validation are preserved. Report food-page-origin candidates, external-origin candidates, existing-evidence overlaps and potential food-page updates separately; do not infer time savings.
