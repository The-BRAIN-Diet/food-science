# PM3 food-evidence reconciliation — 3 November 2026

All 155 extracted Preparation/Synergies blocks in `PM3-food-evidence-extraction.json` were read in this continuation. This is screening, not 155 primary-study appraisals. Original references were followed for the plausible PM3 candidates below; generic culinary advice was not converted to interventions. Food pages were not changed.
