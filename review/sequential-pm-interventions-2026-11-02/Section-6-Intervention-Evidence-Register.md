### PM4 upstream preparation examples — 3 November 2026

Verified cereal phytase treatment and the tested black-tea timing comparison now have separate PM4 upstream iron-provision SOP records. Sources: Hurrell et al. (2003), DOI 10.1093/ajcn/77.5.1213; Ahmad Fuzi et al. (2017), DOI 10.3945/ajcn.117.161364. Endpoint: isotope-estimated non-haem iron absorption. PM4 relationship: shared iron-dependent tyrosine-hydroxylase capacity (PM4-F2). No measured brain iron, noradrenaline or attention benefit; no principal-route superiority. Same experiments reused in PM3 are not additional replication. Intervention SEC: Not yet assessed. Suggested manuscript use: distinguish improved dietary provision from downstream signalling efficacy.
