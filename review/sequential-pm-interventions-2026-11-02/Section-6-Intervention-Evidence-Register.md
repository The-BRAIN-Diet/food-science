#### Diet composition and fuel switching

The missing diet search used the insulin-clamp respiratory-exchange endpoint. Six days at 75% of energy as fat lowered insulin-stimulated carbohydrate oxidation by about 20% in 10 men (10.1210/jc.2006-1592). Six weeks of a healthy diet did not change clamp flexibility versus a Western diet in 40 completers (p = 0.730; 10.1016/j.clnu.2019.12.010). Neither is a flexibility practice. The training record already on the page is unchanged.
