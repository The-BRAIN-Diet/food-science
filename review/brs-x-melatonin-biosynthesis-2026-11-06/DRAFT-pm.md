**Provisional title:** Melatonin Biosynthesis & Availability

**Proposed canonical identity, only after a later approval:** a new Circadian PM, spoken of here as Circadian PM3. The identifier is not assigned.

**Provisional mission:** Sustain melatonin biosynthetic capacity from serotonin through AANAT-mediated acetylation and ASMT-mediated methylation. The operation is the formation of melatonin. It is distinct from light-driven circadian secretion and from melatonin receptor signalling. A change in biosynthetic capacity is not a sleep or ADHD outcome.

## 1. Definition

Pineal melatonin is formed from serotonin. AANAT acetylates serotonin to N-acetylserotonin. ASMT, also called hydroxyindole-O-methyltransferase, methylates N-acetylserotonin to melatonin. Acetyl-CoA supplies the acetyl group. S-adenosylmethionine supplies the methyl group. Light–dark information controls this nocturnal synthesis. That control is not the same claim as a measured shift in the circulating melatonin signal.

## 3. Scientific Principle

**Label:** Serotonin is converted to melatonin by AANAT and ASMT.

**Statement:** AANAT acetylates serotonin to N-acetylserotonin. ASMT methylates N-acetylserotonin to melatonin. The review’s pathway figure names S-adenosylmethionine on that route. AANAT is described as the main rate-limiting enzyme and as controlled by the suprachiasmatic nucleus. Light is described as the dominant environmental control of synthesis.

**Source:** Peuhkuri, Sihvola, and Korpela (2012), `peuhkuri_dietary_2012`, PMC3402070. Pathway section and figure legend, retrieved 6 November 2026.

**Limitation:** This is a narrative review’s pathway description, not a new enzyme assay. No kinetic constants are taken from it. Exogenous melatonin half-life in that review is not biosynthetic capacity.

## 4. Scientific Finding

**Label:** Acute tryptophan depletion lowered nocturnal melatonin secretion.

**Statement:** Eight healthy volunteers underwent active and sham tryptophan depletion in a randomised, double-blind comparison. Acute depletion lowered free and total plasma tryptophan to less than 20% of baseline. Nocturnal melatonin secretion, as area under the curve, was lower after active depletion than after sham depletion in all eight subjects.

**Source:** Zimmermann et al. (1993), DOI 10.1210/jcem.76.5.8496306. Abstract retrieved 6 November 2026 from Europe PMC. This citation is not yet in `BRAIN-diet.bib`. It is not added there in this draft.

**Limitations:**

- The exposure is acute depletion to below 20% of baseline tryptophan, not an ordinary meal and not supplemental tryptophan.
- The abstract does not give the melatonin difference as a concentration or a confidence interval.
- The authors infer a brain-serotonin step. Serotonin itself was not the reported melatonin-study endpoint.
- No sleep, circadian-phase, or ADHD outcome is reported in the retrieved abstract.
- One dataset of eight subjects.

**SEC, draft only, not registered:**

Proposition: acute tryptophan depletion, versus sham depletion, lowered nocturnal melatonin area under the curve in these eight healthy volunteers.

## 6. SOP and Lifestyle

| Candidate | Why it is not admitted here |
|---|---|
| Light–dark exposure | Real control of synthesis, already carried as secretion and phase interventions on Circadian PM1. No second Lifestyle lever. |
| Meal timing | Circadian PM2. The recorded meal delay did not move melatonin timing. |
| Vegetables, barley beer, wine, coffee as melatonin-containing foods | Peuhkuri 2012 treats these as possible sources of ingested melatonin or as unresolved mixtures with vitamins. That is not AANAT or ASMT activity. Unset, and out of this biosynthetic mission until a study separates absorbed melatonin from synthesis. |
| Caffeine | The same review reports conflicting nighttime melatonin results, including a decrease after 200 mg and an increase of 32% in another short study. Designs differ. Unset. No beneficial direction. |
| Nighttime milk | A double-blind elderly comparison used 10–20 ng melatonin from milk. The review itself doubts physiological significance. Not biosynthesis. Unset. |
| Exogenous melatonin capsules | Administration of the hormone, including ADHD sleep studies already in the bibliography. Not precursor biosynthesis. Out of scope. |

No SOP is admitted. No Lifestyle lever is admitted. Empty sections stay empty.
